# Shutara Senjumaru (修多羅千手丸) and her Bankai 娑闥迦羅骸刺絡辻: fact file for a SOUL DUEL character

Episode numbering: Bleach Wiki counts the whole anime, so TYBW episode N is wiki episode 366 + N. Wiki Ep 390 = TYBW 24, Ep 391 = TYBW 25, Ep 392 = TYBW 26, Ep 393 = TYBW 27. The main source is the Bleach Wiki page's wikitext, fetched through the MediaWiki API. It is cited below as [Wiki](https://bleach.fandom.com/wiki/Senjumaru_Shutara), and the manga chapter and page references come from its footnotes.

**Correction to the brief:** the Bankai first appeared in **TYBW episode 26, the finale of Part 2 "The Separation" (訣別譚), aired 30 Sep 2023**. It did not debut in Part 3 "The Conflict" (2024). Part 3's first episode (TYBW 27, "A") continues her fight and shows her death. **The Bankai does not exist in the manga at all. It is anime-original.**

## Who she is, and her base (non-Bankai) combat: canon abilities and limits

### Takeaway
Senjumaru is Squad Zero's "Great Weave Guard" (大織守), who invented the shihakushō. In the manga she fights through tricks: a whole fake palace woven from cloth, clothing she re-tailors onto an enemy that then impales him from inside, and divine-soldier summons. She rarely trades blows, and her needle breaks against Gerard's first slash. The anime gave her a named Shikai, **Shigarami (刺絡)**, and added direct fights: an umbrella that reflects projectiles, sewing a foe to the ground, a rag-doll decoy, healing with cotton, and Shunpo.

### Cited Findings
**Profile**
- Full name 修多羅 千手丸 (Shutara Senjumaru). Title 大織守 (Ōorigami), "Great Weave Guard" (VIZ: "Great Weaver"). Fourth Officer of the Zero Division (零番隊 第四官). Divine General of the North (北方神将, Hoppō Shinshō). Height 158 cm, the shortest of Squad Zero, though she wears very thick okobo. Birthday 1 November. JP voice Rina Satō, EN voice Jeannie Tirado. Manga debut ch. 516, anime debut ep 374 (TYBW 8). — [Wiki](https://bleach.fandom.com/wiki/Senjumaru_Shutara)
- The name 千手丸 means "Thousand Hands" (VIZ: "1,000 Arms"), a reference to how fast she works. Her line to Nianzol: "Swift work is my biggest selling point, you understand. Do not underestimate the name of Senjumaru." (ch. 598 p.16). — [Wiki](https://bleach.fandom.com/wiki/Senjumaru_Shutara)
- Appearance: long black hair, a large golden crescent-moon ornament behind her head with rays coming off it, a shihakushō under a white haori and a white over-robe. **Six long, golden, skeletal prosthetic arms on her back that she uses instead of her own hands** (ch. 517). She moves them seemingly by thought, each can carry a grown Shinigami, and the arms and fingers can stretch a long way, for example to form an umbrella's shaft and ribs. — [Wiki](https://bleach.fandom.com/wiki/Senjumaru_Shutara)
- Personality: polite, serene and composed, though playful or disrespectful at times. She plays several instruments at once as fanfare for Squad Zero's arrival, and she mocks Mayuri after breaking into his lab. She has a dark sense of humour and can be menacing: she threatens to castrate Renji if he won't take off his fundoshi to be measured (ch. 545), and "offers" to sew Uryū's eyes shut. She holds Quincy in contempt as "forsaken by the Soul King." Her first-person pronoun is the archaic 妾 (warawa). — [Wiki](https://bleach.fandom.com/wiki/Senjumaru_Shutara)
- History: she invented the **shihakushō** more than 2,000 years ago, which earned her promotion to the Royal Guard (Kubo's Klub Outside Q&A #149/#389, and the novel *Can't Fear Your Own World*). The robes are black so bloodstains don't matter, and so they work with Ichibe's power over black. Before the Research Institute existed she led an unofficial inventors' group with **Mayuri as her subordinate** (Q&A #548). The "Shutara Scale" (修多羅等級) is a danger scale she created (Q&A #807). — [Wiki](https://bleach.fandom.com/wiki/Senjumaru_Shutara)
- Ōken clothing: she weaves clothes containing the Ōken from her own hair and bones. Yhwach called them the finest clothing a Shinigami can wear. They survive the 72 barrier layers and resist PePe's "The Love." — [Wiki](https://bleach.fandom.com/wiki/Senjumaru_Shutara)

**Manga combat (canon, ch. 597–600)**
- ch. 597: she confronts Yhwach at the Royal Palace with a dozen **Divine Soldiers (神兵)**. Their blades bend away from him. — [Royal Guard vs Wandenreich (Wiki)](https://bleach.fandom.com/wiki/The_Royal_Guard_vs._The_Wandenreich)
- ch. 598: Nianzol Weizol's "The Wind" deflects every enemy attack, even ones he can't see. She had soldiers hidden behind **tapestries that blend into the landscape**. While Nianzol fought the soldiers she **re-tailored his robe with her own fabric** (the Royal Guard insignia appears on his coat) without his noticing, and he cannot take it off. Dozens of spikes then impale him **from inside the garment**. ch. 599: "My bad habit is forgetting to remove my sewing needles from garments." — [Royal Guard vs Wandenreich (Wiki)](https://bleach.fandom.com/wiki/The_Royal_Guard_vs._The_Wandenreich)
- ch. 599: Gerard **shatters her sewing needle** with one slash of Hoffnung and draws blood. She calls a giant **Second-Level Divine Soldier**, which Pernida crushes into a ball, and Lille shoots a hole through her head. — [Royal Guard vs Wandenreich (Wiki)](https://bleach.fandom.com/wiki/The_Royal_Guard_vs._The_Wandenreich)
- ch. 600, the reveal: everything since the Quincy arrived took place inside **a fake Soul King Palace woven from cloth**, cities included. The Senjumaru who died was fabric. The real one is unharmed, and the stall gave Hikifune time to finish the **Cage of Life**. Ichibe hid the real palace. — [Royal Guard vs Wandenreich (Wiki)](https://bleach.fandom.com/wiki/The_Royal_Guard_vs._The_Wandenreich)
- After that the manga shows only Nimaiya fighting. The rest of Squad Zero lose to the Auswählen-empowered Schutzstaffel **off-screen**, and none of the other four shows a Shikai or Bankai in the manga. — [CBR](https://www.cbr.com/bleach-tybw-anime-overpowered-squad-0/); [Sportskeeda](https://sportskeeda.com/anime/bleach-tybw-part-2-finale-episode-25-26-yhwach-vs-ichibei-begins-senjumaru-reveals-bankai)

**Shikai: Shigarami (刺絡, "Piercing Entanglement"), named only in the anime (TYBW 26)**
- It looks like a **sewing needle**. The release command and sealed form are unknown. She can stab with it (at the heart or neck) through her skeletal arms, but mostly she uses it to sew. — [Wiki](https://bleach.fandom.com/wiki/Senjumaru_Shutara)
- Ability: it creates and controls fabric woven from the **red Reishi thread that runs endlessly from the needle's eye** (TYBW 27). Uses: (a) tapestries that fall to enclose a space and show a realistic false scene (manga ch. 598, 600); (b) instantly re-tailoring an enemy's clothes, with fatal spikes hidden inside (manga ch. 598–599); (c) **sewing an enemy to the ground** with many threads to pin them (TYBW 26); (d) an **umbrella** woven between her six arms in seconds that blocks Uryū's Licht Regen, which she then splits and **fires back as Reishi tendrils** (TYBW 25); (e) filling an ally's wounds with **cotton and thread that rebuild lost flesh** (she heals Nimaiya after Lille's X-Axis, TYBW 26); (f) a **rag-doll copy of herself** that takes hits for her (TYBW 26). — [Wiki](https://bleach.fandom.com/wiki/Senjumaru_Shutara); [CBR](https://www.cbr.com/bleach-tybw-anime-overpowered-squad-0/)
- She also shows expert Shunpo and keeps pace with Uryū (anime). Her Reiatsu is yellow. In TYBW 25 she makes an effortless fight of Uryū, blocking his Blut Vene hands with rapid needle strikes. — [Wiki](https://bleach.fandom.com/wiki/Senjumaru_Shutara); [Sportskeeda](https://sportskeeda.com/anime/bleach-tybw-part-2-finale-episode-25-26-yhwach-vs-ichibei-begins-senjumaru-reveals-bankai)
- Other manga-canon utility: she carries wounded people in large translucent orbs (ch. 517), and she builds a **floating spiral staircase** for Ichigo (ch. 555). — [Wiki](https://bleach.fandom.com/wiki/Senjumaru_Shutara)

### Inferences
- Her canon identity is **deception and preparation**: fake scenery, pre-tailored traps and summoned proxies. She is not a brawler, and when she is caught in a straight fight (Gerard's slash) she loses. That fits a setplay character with a weak neutral and a strong setup.
- The six arms are her silhouette and should be her animation language: everything happens at arm's reach, fast and many-handed. Her own body stays still, perched on okobo.

### Gaps
- The manga never names or releases her Shikai. The name "Shigarami" and every Shikai feat above except the tapestries and the tailoring come from the anime.
- I could not confirm the exact manga chapter where Squad Zero is shown defeated off-screen. CBR and Sportskeeda say only that it happened off-screen.

## The Bankai: 娑闥迦羅骸刺絡辻 (Shatatsu Karagara Shigarami no Tsuji), anime-original

### Takeaway
It is anime-only (TYBW 26, 30 Sep 2023, Part 2 finale). It can only be released after the other three Divine Generals kill themselves to break the **Blood Oath Seal** (血盟の封印). Her full power shakes all three worlds. A torii rises and becomes a giant golden loom inside a darkened space draped in red cloth. The loom then weaves **one patterned bolt of cloth per enemy**. Each is unrolled in turn into its own deadly environment (mirrors, spiked iron, black sand, ice, fire, drain), and the finished pieces are cut off with the victims sealed inside. The Bankai is overwhelming, but Yhwach's The Almighty lets Uryū break free. Uryū's Antithesis then reverses their positions: she ends up trapped in her own Bankai and dies.

### Cited Findings
**Name and meaning**
- 娑闥迦羅骸刺絡辻, read しゃたつ からがら しがらみのつじ, Shatatsu Karagara Shigarami no Tsuji. — [Pixiv 百科 search listing](https://dic.pixiv.net/a/%E5%A8%91%E9%97%A5%E8%BF%A6%E7%BE%85%E9%AA%B8%E5%88%BA%E7%B5%A1%E8%BE%BB); [Wiki](https://bleach.fandom.com/wiki/Senjumaru_Shutara)
- Translations: the Wiki gives "Crossing of Sahā's Gate, Kāla's Corpse and Piercing Entanglements." Game Rant gives "Gate of Saha, Kala's Corpse at the Crossroads of Thorny Entanglement." The parts: 娑 = Sahā (the world of suffering outside nirvana), 闥 = gate, 迦羅 = Kāla (time and death), 骸 = corpse or skeleton, 刺絡 = Shigarami (her Shikai's name), 辻 = crossroads. — [Wiki](https://bleach.fandom.com/wiki/Senjumaru_Shutara); [Game Rant](https://gamerant.com/bleach-thousand-year-blood-war-shutara-senjumaru-bankai-explained/)
- Game Rant also reads Buddhist, Hindu and Shinto references into it: Kagutsuchi, Bhairava, and the "red carpet as a crossroads" motif. — [Game Rant](https://gamerant.com/bleach-thousand-year-blood-war-shutara-senjumaru-bankai-explained/) *(This is interpretation, not official.)*

**Precondition and cost (the canon limit)**
- Each Royal Guard member has enough Reiryoku to destabilise the Three Worlds, so their power is sealed. Senjumaru's life is linked to the three other Divine Generals through the Blood Oath Seal, and **one of them can use full power only if the other three are dead**. In TYBW 26, Nimaiya, Kirinji and Hikifune take their own lives, and she releases the Bankai. The release causes tremors in all Three Worlds ("make heaven and earth across the Three Worlds tremble"). — [Wiki](https://bleach.fandom.com/wiki/Senjumaru_Shutara); [ComicBook.com](https://comicbook.com/anime/news/bleach-thousand-year-blood-war-senjumaru-bankai-anime-original/)
- She keeps the Shikai needle as an ordinary weapon while the Bankai is active (TYBW 27). — [Wiki](https://bleach.fandom.com/wiki/Senjumaru_Shutara)

**Visuals**
- The area turns dark and a **torii-like gate** rises from the ground. She raises her skeletal arms toward it as if praying. The gate shimmers and becomes a **huge golden loom** that pours out endless red *tanmono* (narrow bolts of cloth). One rolls out like a **red carpet** over smaller cloths that connect to tapestry looms on both sides. More red cloth surrounds the scene **like a barrier wall**, and patterned bolts hang from above. — [Wiki](https://bleach.fandom.com/wiki/Senjumaru_Shutara); [Game Rant](https://gamerant.com/bleach-thousand-year-blood-war-shutara-senjumaru-bankai-explained/)
- Japanese viewers called the staging "lavish and horror-like" (演出すげぇ豪華でホラーだった), and "千手丸の卍解" trended on X. Futaman described it as "terrifying yet beautiful" (恐ろしくも美しく). — [ORICON headline](https://www.oricon.co.jp/news/2296869/full/); [Futaman](https://futaman.futabanet.jp/articles/-/130726?page=1); [ABEMA Times](https://times.abema.tv/articles/-/10097749)

**The ability**
- She controls the space inside the Bankai through her cloth, "weaving and sealing victims' fates within." Each cloth has its own pattern and power, **designed to counter that opponent's traits**. **The number of cloths matches the number of enemies**, and the technique's name changes to match. — [Wiki](https://bleach.fandom.com/wiki/Senjumaru_Shutara)
- **死出六色浮文機 Shide no Rokushiki Ukimon no Hata**, "Loom of the Six-Coloured Floating Patterns of Death": six tanmono for six opponents (Lille, Askin, Pernida, Gerard, Haschwalth and Uryū). **One hank is unravelled at a time**, creating an environment that wears down its target as the cloth wraps them. She then **cuts all the finished pieces off the loom with the victims sealed inside**. It beats the Auswählen-empowered Schutzstaffel and appears to ignore the usual one-element limit on elemental Zanpakutō. — [Wiki](https://bleach.fandom.com/wiki/Senjumaru_Shutara)
- The six "unravellings" (綛解かば, "if one hank is untied…"), each with a chanted line:
  1. **一綛解かば 万朶の眼 (Banra no Me), "Eyes of Many Branches"** (Lille): a purple cloth with rhombus and eye patterns that grows real eyes and forms eight mirrors. The mirrors **reflect the victim's own attacks back onto him**. Chant: "The eyes be crushed, of those who stare back."
  2. **二綛解かば 刃金のよろい (Hagane no Yoroi), "Armor of Blade Metal"** (Askin): a gold cloth patterned with barbed scales that becomes **spiked metal sheets cut in her likeness and closes like an iron maiden**. Chant: "There shall be nothing, to stand dressed in."
  3. **三綛解かば 黒砂の腸 (Kokusa no Harawata), "Bowels of Black Sand"** (Pernida): a black spiral-fractal cloth that becomes **a pit of whirling black sand that swallows the target**. Chant: "To be pulled in so, shall be fatal."
  4. **四綛解かば 凍てつく褥 (Itetsuku Shitone), "Freezing Bed Linens"** (Gerard): a blue snowflake cloth that brings arctic cold and **binds and freezes the target solid**. Chant: "To enter a place, in which never to wake again."
  5. **五綛解かば 焼野原 (Yakenohara), "Burning Field"** (Haschwalth): a procession of red cloths showing burning black trees that **line a corridor and burn the target to ash**. Chant: "Until turned to ashes, there shall be no escape."
  6. **六綛解かば 闇夜の星よ (Yamiyo no Hoshiyo), "Star of the Dark Night"** (Uryū): a dark-blue cloth bearing the Wandenreich emblem that copies Auswählen and **drains the target's energy**. Chant: "To be caught in the end, by the star of death."
  — [Wiki](https://bleach.fandom.com/wiki/Senjumaru_Shutara)
- Each cloth seems to foreshadow its victim's later manga fate: Lille beaten by Kyōraku/Ise's mirrors, Askin by three people, Pernida by the Kurotsuchi ("black soil"), Gerard fighting Hitsugaya's ice, Haschwalth dying against Bazz-B's fire, and Uryū tied to Auswählen and the Still Silver. The Wiki marks this as unconfirmed. — [Wiki](https://bleach.fandom.com/wiki/Senjumaru_Shutara)
- In TYBW 27 Uryū breaks free after Yhwach uses The Almighty. She weaves **new cloths with Hollow designs that release real Huge Hollows** at him, then seals him again and vows to wipe out the Quincy line. Uryū enters Vollständig and uses **Antithesis to swap their conditions, trapping her in her own Bankai**, then shoots a Heilig Pfeil through her heart. Her death breaks down the Bankai and frees the Schutzstaffel. — [Wiki](https://bleach.fandom.com/wiki/Senjumaru_Shutara); [Royal Guard vs Wandenreich (Wiki)](https://bleach.fandom.com/wiki/The_Royal_Guard_vs._The_Wandenreich)
- Famous line in TYBW 27: "Are you feeling it stir inside your veins, Uryū Ishida? … Your kind has outlived your time. I will eradicate your bloodline here and now." — [Wiki](https://bleach.fandom.com/wiki/Senjumaru_Shutara)

### Inferences
- In canon the Bankai is **a domain plus a queue**: one space, several prepared "sentences", carried out one at a time in a fixed order. That structure maps well onto a fighting-game timer or sequence.
- Its canon weaknesses translate straight into mechanics. (1) It depends on her staying in control: The Almighty broke it and Antithesis turned it back on her. (2) It is prepared and specific to its target: each cloth must be woven before it is unravelled. (3) The cost is paid up front, the lives of the other three.

### Gaps
- Nothing official says how Kubo was involved in this particular Bankai (its name, design or abilities). Futaman describes the anime as a whole as supervised by Kubo (久保帯人監修) but credits nothing specific. Do not claim Kubo designed it.
- The Bankai has no known release chant. The "袖振るだけで三界天地を震えさす" in one YouTube title looks like a paraphrase and is unverified.

## Design hooks for SOUL DUEL (base vs awakened as different playstyles, and keeping a trap character fair)

### Takeaway
Base form: **"the Tailor", a tag-and-detonate and decoy setplay character**, the canon of the Nianzol kill, the fake palace and the umbrella. Awakened: **"the Loom", a domain with a fixed queue of six unravellings**. The whole playstyle changes, but her damage output doesn't. Fairness comes from canon's own limits: every trap is prepared and telegraphed, the domain holds a single queue, and hitting her breaks the weave.

### Cited Findings (project context)
- SOUL DUEL already has a hazard system, with fixed hazard blockstun, a hazard hit guard of 12, perfect Hoho timing against hazard hit windows, and the precedent that Kenpachi's KUKAN-GIRI **rift closes if he is hit before it cuts** and can be left as a whiffed trap. There is one awakening per match, from the Fighting Spirit gauge. Awakening changes the Kikon count from 2 to 3. — /media/8tsp/projects/ai-cl-game-test/docs/duel/DUEL_DESIGN.md §2, §4, §6.2
- Brave Souls precedent: one element per input; a Divine Soldier summon; a barrier; the "Bad Habits" garment. — sources above

### Inferences (proposals, not canon)
**Base form, "Shigarami" (manga + anime Shikai):**
- **Tailoring (a tag):** her J/K strings put stitches on the opponent's clothing. Hits add stitches, blocks add fewer. Her O ender or a specific K3 **pulls the needles**: each stitch detonates as a spike (the "bad habit" and the Nianzol kill). The stitches fade on a timer, so the opponent's counterplay is to break contact. This is a delayed-damage setplay, not burst.
- **Divine Soldier (a summon/hazard):** a stationary or slow proxy that attacks on a delay (manga ch. 597/599; Brave Souls SA3). One at a time. It dies to a single hit, as Pernida crushed the giant one.
- **Umbrella (a projectile catch):** a short-window guard that stores ranged hits and **fires them back as tendrils** (TYBW 25). It is useful against Yamamoto's fire and ranged windows. A melee Breaker breaks it.
- **Rag-doll substitute:** a guard-crush escape that uses meter (the decoy from TYBW 26 and the fake death from manga ch. 600). Keep it to one charge per reset.
- **Sew to the ground:** a short bind on a counter-hit only (TYBW 26).
- Weakness: a poor straight exchange (Gerard shattered her needle), so short-range buttons, slow startup and low guard health.

**Awakened, "娑闥迦羅骸刺絡辻" (anime Bankai):**
- The awakening cinematic: three candles go out (the Blood Oath), a torii rises, the loom opens, the red carpet rolls out, and the arena walls turn to red cloth. The domain lasts to the end of the match, like Yamamoto and Kenpachi (one awakening, no timer).
- **The loom queue:** the HUD shows six hanks in order: 1 mirror eyes, 2 blade-iron, 3 black sand, 4 frozen bed, 5 burning field, 6 dark star. Her special input **unravels the next hank** as a zone hazard. Only **one unravelling is active at a time**, and the next is always visible, so the opponent can read and prepare for it. Suggested roles:
  1. Mirrors reflect projectiles and punish her opponent's ranged hits (Lille).
  2. A spike ring punishes block-mashing inside it and drains the guard gauge (Askin).
  3. The sand pit pulls toward its centre, which is spacing control (Pernida).
  4. Ice freezes the target on hit inside the zone (Gerard).
  5. A fire corridor is a line zone with chip (Haschwalth).
  6. The star drains Reiatsu or Fighting Spirit (Uryū, Auswählen).
  The zones are not stronger than base-form damage. The awakening trades the base form's tag-and-detonate for **terrain control**.
- **Sealing Kikon (O):** "cuts the finished piece from the loom." The Kikon sequence wraps the opponent in the hank currently active, which matches the canon finisher (Shide no Rokushiki).
- **The canon break rule:** when she is hit cleanly, the unravelling in progress **tears** (the Almighty and Antithesis logic, the same as Kenpachi's rift closing). This is the main counterplay: rush the weaver. A Soul Break or Burst could "reverse" a zone once (Antithesis) so that it hits her. That's optional, a flavour hook.
- **Hollow cloth (optional):** an anime-only extra from TYBW 27, where the cloths release Huge Hollows. It could be a Kikon or SP2 flourish. Don't make it a seventh zone.

**Fairness rules for a trap/zone character in a 3D fighter:**
- Every hazard has a visible telegraph, meaning cloth that unrolls before it is active, and a fixed lifetime. Only one weave is active at a time.
- **3D sidestep/Flash Step must defeat linear zones** (the fire corridor, a spike line). Circular zones (sand, spikes) must be escapable in a single Flash Step from the centre.
- The trap owner is vulnerable while setting up, since weaving has recovery. Being hit cancels the setup.
- Stitches and tags decay, and the defender can see them (a visible thread on the victim).
- The traps don't stack into loops: a hazard hit gives a fixed blockstun and hitstun that can't be comboed indefinitely. The existing `*hazard-blockstun*` pattern already handles this.

### Gaps
- There are no canon numbers (range, duration) for any of her techniques. All tuning is design work.
- Whether the Blood Oath sacrifice should appear in the awakening flavour (three candles) is a tone decision for the user. It is canon, but grim.
