# Lille Barro (リジェ・バロ), Sternritter "X", The X-Axis (万物貫通): fact file for a SOUL DUEL character

**How this was researched, and why it matters for the confidence flags.** This session's network policy blocked every page fetch: bleach.fandom.com, Game Rant, Sportskeeda, pixiv, atwiki, ciatr, renote, TV Tropes, Wikipedia, blogspot, daddyjim.ai and the Wayback Machine all returned EGRESS_BLOCKED. Everything below therefore comes from **web-search result summaries**: the search engine quotes and paraphrases the Bleach Wiki and its sub-pages ("Shunsui Kyōraku vs. Lille Barro", "Nanao Ise vs. Lille Barro", "Trompete", "Sabaki no Kōmyō", the episode pages), the Villains Wiki, Japanese fan wikis (アニヲタWiki, ニコニコ大百科, pixiv), Game Rant, Sportskeeda, ANN and episode reviews. Where two or more independent summaries agree, a claim is marked **[high]**. A claim from a single summary is **[med]**. A claim from one weak or contradictory summary is **[low]**. Chapter numbers come from those summaries, and **I could not check them against page footnotes**. Before anything is quoted in the manual, open the Bleach Wiki "Lille Barro" page in a browser and check the chapter numbers.

Episode numbering: the Bleach Wiki counts the whole anime, so TYBW episode N is wiki episode 366 + N. For example, TYBW 37 "SHADOWS GONE" is overall episode 403 **[high]**.

**Corrections to the brief:**
- **"Gottes" / "God's eye" is not an official name** that I could find. The official names are *Jilliel* (神の裁き, "Judgement of God") for the Vollständig, and *Trompete* (神の喇叭, "Trumpet of God") and *Sabaki no Kōmyō* (裁きの光明) for the techniques. The "Cherubim form" (Game Rant) and the Chinese fan label 神之眼 are fan names. A VS Battles thread states outright that "second form" is not an official name either. What *is* canon about eyes: the X-Axis is gated by his **left eye**, the second form has an **owl's eyes**, and one of its signature lines is about his **eyes drying out**. That is probably the memory behind "God's eye".
- **Kyōraku did not exploit a "tangible while attacking" window.** I found no source for such a rule. In Jilliel, Lille is **constantly** intangible. Kyōraku hurt him through **rule-based effects that never touch him physically**: the four acts of the Bankai, which share wounds, share an illness, drown him and cut his throat with a thread. Nanao hurt him by **reflecting his own power back into him** with Shinken Hakkyōken.
- **He is defeated in stages, not in one blow.** The Bankai beheads him (ch. ~649). He revives in an owl-headed second form (ch. 650). His own Trompete, reflected by Nanao's Hakkyōken, shatters him (ch. 653). He falls apart into bird-like remnants that undead Kira and Aizen cut down (ch. 654 onward). A light novel confirms that the last remnants were cleared up after the war.

## 1. Who he is, appearance, Diagramm

### Takeaway
He is the leader of Yhwach's Schutzstaffel and the **first Quincy ever given a Schrift**. He is a dark-skinned sniper with a crew cut, keeps his left eye shut under a crosshair mark, and wears a green fur bicorne. He fights with a long black sniper rifle, *Diagramm*, whose barrel ends in a cross-shaped muzzle brake. Visually he is "a priest-soldier with a rifle". There is nothing in his base form for melee.

### Cited Findings
- Name リジェ・バロ, *Lille Barro*. Sternritter designation **"X": The X-Axis (万物貫通, "Penetration of All Things" or "pierces all things")**. Leader of the **Schutzstaffel (親衛隊)**, Yhwach's elite guard **[high]**. He says he is the first Quincy on whom Yhwach bestowed a Schrift, "his ultimate creation, closer to God than any other man" **[high]**. He uses the first-person pronoun **ボク (boku)** **[med]**. — [Bleach Wiki](https://bleach.fandom.com/wiki/Lille_Barro), [Villains Wiki](https://villains.fandom.com/wiki/Lille_Barro), [ニコニコ大百科](https://dic.nicovideo.jp/a/%E3%83%AA%E3%82%B8%E3%82%A7%E3%83%BB%E3%83%90%E3%83%AD)
- Profile: **182 cm, born 11 April** (Bleach Wiki infobox, via search) **[med]**. *Conflict:* another summary of the wiki gives "five feet nine inches", which is 175 cm **[low]**. Use 182 cm and treat it as unverified.
- Voices: JP **Satoshi Hino (日野聡)**, EN **Evan Michael Lee** **[med]** (one summary, which agrees with MAL character 114967's listing). — [MyAnimeList](https://myanimelist.net/character/114967)
- Manga debut **ch. 599**, where he shoots (what turns out to be) Senjumaru's cloth decoy through the head **[med]**. — [Bleach Wiki](https://bleach.fandom.com/wiki/Lille_Barro); see also senjumaru.md
- **Appearance (Bleach Wiki text, via search) [med–high]:** youthful-looking, dark skin, muscular build, black eyes, **short white hair cut almost to a crew cut**. He has a **crosshair-shaped mark over his left eye, which he usually keeps shut**. A long white cloak over white gloves embroidered with a **winged X**. A light sleeveless shirt with a **dark-green furred pauldron on the right shoulder**. Dark-green trousers with white leggings that have cut-outs near the calves and attach to the shirt. Light shoes. A **dark-green furred bicorne** with a small Wandenreich emblem on each side. — [Bleach Wiki](https://bleach.fandom.com/wiki/Lille_Barro), [Villains Wiki](https://villains.fandom.com/wiki/Lille_Barro)
- **Diagramm (ディアグラム), his Spirit Weapon:** "a large sniper rifle made of black metal, which he carries on his back underneath his cloak. The slender barrel features a **black, cross-shaped muzzle brake** at its tip" **[med–high]**. It has a **scope and a dial**: in ch. 644 he adjusts the dial and looks through the scope before gunning down Kyōraku's subordinates in Wahrwelt **[med]**. One summary says the rifle is "attached to his right arm" **[low]**; that contradicts the wiki text, so ignore it. In ch. 645 Kyōraku **severs most of Diagramm's barrel** **[high]**. — [Bleach Wiki](https://bleach.fandom.com/wiki/Lille_Barro), [Shunsui vs Lille (Wiki)](https://bleach.fandom.com/wiki/Shunsui_Ky%C5%8Draku_vs._Lille_Barro)

### Inferences
- His silhouette has three readable elements: the **long rifle line**, the **bicorne**, and the **one shut eye with its crosshair**. In SOUL DUEL's ink style, the eye mark is the best "state" indicator, open versus shut (see §2).
- A **182 cm** frame is close to Ichigo's and Kenpachi's band. That works for the body/brush rig without a special skeleton until the Vollständig.

### Gaps
- No front/back reference was fetched. The colour of the shirt and boots ("light-coloured") is vague. Before modelling, check the TYBW Part 3 key art and ep 35 stills by eye.

## 2. Base-form abilities, in order of appearance

### Takeaway
**The X-Axis = whatever lies on the straight line from the muzzle to the target is pierced, and no barrier or guard stops it.** It is presented as a sniper's shot or a beam that effectively arrives at once. **The "quintessence" (本質) = with both eyes open he applies the X-Axis to his own body, so attacks pass through him.** A self-imposed rule governs this: the left eye stays shut unless he is in crisis, and **once he has had to open it three times in one battle he may keep it open** and then releases his Vollständig.

### Cited Findings
- **Ch. 599:** a long-range headshot on Senjumaru's decoy **[med]**.
- **Ch. 601–602 (Royal Guard round two):** Nimaiya had cut the Schutzstaffel down. Yhwach's **Auswählen revives and strengthens them**. Lille fires at Nimaiya while **Kirinji and Hikifune shield him**, and "no amount of barriers will stop his shot": Nimaiya is **shot through the heart** **[med]**. Another summary: "punched shots clean through the blades of two Royal Guards to strike the man they shielded" **[med]**. Lille denies that his bullets became stronger: he is "simply using a power he could not access until now" **[med]**. — [Royal Guard vs Wandenreich (Wiki)](https://bleach.fandom.com/wiki/The_Royal_Guard_vs._The_Wandenreich), [Chapter 611 (Wiki)](https://bleach.fandom.com/wiki/Chapter_611)
- **What the X-Axis does:** "pierce anything that comes along the straight line in-between the target and his sniper rifle's muzzle". "The true nature is not firing bullets but **instantly penetrating any object along the rifle's line of sight**, making dodging impossible and bypassing all barriers" **[med]** (fan wiki paraphrase). In the manga he fires bullet-shaped **Heilig Pfeil** of compressed Reishi **[med]**. Niconico: "pierces everything on the line of fire, no defence can stop it" **[high]**. — [Villains Wiki](https://villains.fandom.com/wiki/Lille_Barro), [ニコニコ大百科](https://dic.nicovideo.jp/a/%E3%83%AA%E3%82%B8%E3%82%A7%E3%83%BB%E3%83%90%E3%83%AD)
- **Is there a delay?** No source mentions a delay or a lingering hole. Every description says *instant* along a line. The only build-up shown is his **aiming** (the dial and scope, ch. 644) **[med]**.
- **Ch. 644 (TYBW 34):** he snipes the Shinigami in Wahrwelt from a distance (he was also shown watching Uryū through the scope in TYBW 34) **[med]**. — [Sportskeeda TYBW 34 summary](https://sportskeeda.com/anime/bleach-tybw-episode-34-mayuri-vs-pernida-begins-identity-soul-king-s-left-arm-revealed)
- **Ch. 645 "Don't Chase a Shadow" (TYBW 35), Kyōraku's Shikai games against him [high]:**
  - **Daruma-san ga Koronda:** Kyōraku leaves **solidified Reiatsu** where he stood. Lille "shoots" it, and Kyōraku appears behind him and cuts most of Diagramm's barrel off. Lille's deadpan reply, 「成程、チョコラテ・イングレスか」 ("I see, *Chocolate Inglés*", the Spanish-speaking name of the same children's game), became a well-known meme in Japan **[high]**.
  - **Kageokuri:** Kyōraku projects an **afterimage**, and Lille shoots that instead **[high]**.
  - **Kageoni:** when Kyōraku strikes up at him from a shadow, Lille **leaps clear on the first try**. Kyōraku notes that Lille is the first opponent ever to dodge it first time **[high]**.
  - **Takaoni** (whoever is higher wins) is also used in the exchange **[med]**.
  - These tricks put Lille in crisis three times. **Each time he opens his left eye, the blade passes through him** **[high]**. — [Shunsui vs Lille (Wiki)](https://bleach.fandom.com/wiki/Shunsui_Ky%C5%8Draku_vs._Lille_Barro), [User blog ch. 645](https://bleach.fandom.com/wiki/User_blog:Xilinoc/Ch._645_-_Don't_Chase_a_Shadow)
- **The eye rule (ch. 645–646) [high]:** "His left eye must be kept shut until he falls into crisis during battle, for him opening both eyes would be **unfair to the 'sinners'** facing him. Only when forced to open his left eye **three times** during a single battle is Lille permitted to keep it open for its remainder." With both eyes open he can use the **quintessence** of the X-Axis on his own body: "his shot penetrates his opponent's body and **his body penetrates his opponent's sword**". In the base form each opening gives **short-lived** intangibility **[med]**. After the third opening he gives the "first Quincy… closer to God… tantamount to heresy" speech and releases the Vollständig. — [Bleach Wiki](https://bleach.fandom.com/wiki/Lille_Barro), [ニコニコ大百科](https://dic.nicovideo.jp/a/%E3%83%AA%E3%82%B8%E3%82%A7%E3%83%BB%E3%83%90%E3%83%AD), [Villains Wiki](https://villains.fandom.com/wiki/Lille_Barro)
- **Blut, Hirenkyaku, melee:** the summaries list only the generic Sternritter kit (Hirenkyaku for speed, Blut Vene for durability) **[low]**. **I found no named melee technique**, and he never trades blows in the canon summaries. His defence is evasion (the Kageoni leap) or phasing.

### Inferences
- His canon "defence" is **not blocking**. It is (a) never being in reach, (b) reflex dodges, and (c) a **limited-use phase** (three eye-openings). That is a meter design waiting to happen.
- The three-openings rule is unusually game-like: it is a **counter that unlocks the transformation**. Canon itself ties his "awakening" to being pressured. It is not a free button.

### Gaps
- The exact phrasing of his X-Axis explanation (whether it is "the line between muzzle and target" or "the line of the X-axis") in the VIZ text. The page numbers for each eye-opening.

## 3. Vollständig: Jilliel (神の裁き, ジリエル, "Judgement of God"), ch. 646 onward

### Takeaway
It looks like an **armless, cocooned angel**. He has **eight large wings, each pierced by three holes (24 muzzles)**, a cream-white carapace robe up to the mouth, a wide thin halo, and a bigger face tattoo. Powers: flight, **many X-Axis shots fired at once from the wing holes**, a combined blast that cuts through city blocks, and **constant intangibility**, which canon describes as "an enemy that no weapon or spell can wound", Kidō included.

### Cited Findings
- **Name:** Jilliel, written 神の裁き with ジリエル furigana, "Judgement of God" **[high]**. — [Bleach Wiki](https://bleach.fandom.com/wiki/Lille_Barro), [Game Rant](https://gamerant.com/bleach-tybw-lille-barro-vollstandig-explained/)
- **Look [high]:** "**eight wings with three holes each**, a large carapace-like robe covering his body below the mouth, a more elaborate tattoo on his face, and a wide and thin Heiligenschein". Japanese: 「4対の羽を持つ天使のようなフォルム」(an angel-like form with four pairs of wings). A Chinese summary adds that **his arms are replaced by the wings** and his body is "cocoon-like" **[med]**. This fits the second form "regrowing the arms he had lost" **[med]**. Wing colour: the anime gives the Jilliel Reiatsu a **green** glow that **shifts to gold** in the second form **[med]** (episode review). Brave Souls describes "eight golden wings" **[med]**. Fans call this form the "winged chair" **[low, fan nickname]**. — [ciatr (search summary)](https://ciatr.jp/topics/338893), [Sohu](https://www.sohu.com/a/904428868_120352010), [Anime Explained ep 37 review](https://www.animeexplained.com/explained/bleach-thousand-year-blood-war-episode-37-review/)

"Each wing having three holes that fire **instantaneous** shots" (ch. 646 review). **Gathering energy in all 24 holes** gives a blast "with enough force to sever part of the city below" **[med]**. — [Shunsui vs Lille (Wiki)](https://bleach.fandom.com/wiki/Shunsui_Ky%C5%8Draku_vs._Lille_Barro), [tumblr ch. 646 review](https://www.tumblr.com/dailyanimeart/130679038350/bleach-646-sees-lille-barro-vs-shunsui-as-their)
- **Sabaki no Kōmyō (裁きの光明), "Ray/Light of Judgement":** the Japanese summaries say the wings fire light that carries the X-Axis, called 裁きの光明 **[med]**. One summary places it in the first Jilliel ("Lille fires Sabaki no Kōmyō at Shunsui's location, destroying several buildings") **[med]**. **Conflict:** the Bleach Wiki technique page (via search) says that **in the *true* (second) form** he "makes a **chopping gesture with his arm** and fires **thin waves of golden light**" that pierce cityscapes, leaving explosions along their path **[med]**. Both versions are piercing light. Decide per form when designing. — [Sabaki no Kōmyō (Wiki)](https://bleach.fandom.com/wiki/Sabaki_no_K%C5%8Dmy%C5%8D), [ciatr](https://ciatr.jp/topics/338893)

Kyōraku, "bloodied and without his hat", is chased across the Wahrwelt rooftops "by an enemy that **no weapon or spell can wound**". **No source I found states a "solid while attacking" exception.** — [Villains Wiki](https://villains.fandom.com/wiki/Lille_Barro), [Shunsui vs Lille (Wiki)](https://bleach.fandom.com/wiki/Shunsui_Ky%C5%8Draku_vs._Lille_Barro)

### Inferences
- Canon has **two ways to beat the phase**: (1) **rules and status effects** that ignore physical contact (shared wounds, an illness or DOT, a drowning field), and (2) **his own attack turned back on him** (Hakkyōken, §4). Neither is "hit him while he shoots". A fair game version can still add a **commitment window** as a design concession (see §8), but label it SOUL DUEL's rule, not canon.
- Flight did not save him from the Bankai's water. A domain or field effect is the thematic counter to an airborne zoner.

### Gaps
- Exactly which Bankai act took his Vollständig damage on which page, and whether Act 2 also afflicted Kyōraku. The Wiki fight page has this, but I couldn't open it.

## 4. The second form ("true form" of Jilliel), Trompete, and the real defeat

### Takeaway
**What Yhwach "gave" him is not a separate gift.** As the first person to receive a Schrift, Lille has had Yhwach's power growing inside him for longer than anyone else, until his Vollständig turns him into "something more powerful than a human". He says weapons can't kill him and even beheading can't. **Look:** the headless body floats up, its Reiatsu turning from green to gold, and regrows a **barn-owl head** on a long serpentine neck: slicked-back fair hair over an owl's eyes, nose and beak. He has a small spiked halo, long centaur-like legs, and regrown, larger arms. **Moves:** golden light waves (Sabaki no Kōmyō), light from his eyes, and **Trompete (神の喇叭)**: he mimes a trumpet at his beak, a giant golden trumpet forms overhead, and he "blows" a city-erasing X-Axis blast. **Defeat:** Nanao's **Shinken Hakkyōken** absorbs and scatters "the power of a god", then **reflects Trompete back into him**. The blast cuts a thin strip through his body, takes his left arm and a set of wings, and **destroys his halo**. He falls and shatters into bird-like remnants, which **undead Kira** (Wabisuke) and **Aizen** cut down. A novel confirms the last remnants were wiped out after the war.

### Cited Findings
- **Revival (ch. 650, TYBW 37) [high]:** "Lille's headless body teleports into the air with Jilliel still active and shifting in colour **from green to golden**, where Lille's face begins reforming from energy." Entering the second form "**regenerates any body parts that Lille has lost**." — [Bleach Wiki](https://bleach.fandom.com/wiki/Lille_Barro), [dailyanimeart ch. 650 (title)](https://dailyanimeart.com/2015/11/05/nanaos-zanpakuto-kyokotsu-lilles-evolution-bleach-650/)
- **Why he can do this [med–high]:** "As the first Quincy to have been given their Schrift by Yhwach, the latter's power had **grown excessively within Lille over time**, to the point where his Quincy: Vollständig transforms his body into something more powerful than a Human." Another summary says his soul "evolved to the level of a God" **[low–med]**. — [Bleach Wiki (search summary)](https://bleach.fandom.com/wiki/Lille_Barro)
- **Look [high]:** "an angelic, bird-like being with a very curved but thin, lanky build… a new, fair-skinned head with **slicked-back hair**… but with the **eyes, nose, and beak of an owl**. His Heiligenschein is **drastically reduced in size and gains spikes**, and Lille's neck is elongated, resembling the body of a **snake with fur on its back**." "His shoes greatly extend, making him look like a **centaur**, and he regrows the arms he had lost in his Vollständig, but **much larger**." Japanese: 「メンフクロウと人が合わさった化け物」(a monster combining a barn owl and a human). Game Rant calls it the "Cherubim form" (not official), and fans call it a "humanoid chicken". In the anime his **voice is digitally processed** in this form, and the anime adds a **first-person shot from inside his eyes** as he stares into Nanao's mirrors (anime-original) **[med]**. — [Sportskeeda (owl design)](https://www.sportskeeda.com/anime/why-lille-barro-turn-owl-like-angel-bleach-tybw-his-second-form-s-design-explored), [Anime Explained ep 37](https://www.animeexplained.com/explained/bleach-thousand-year-blood-war-episode-37-review/), [ciatr](https://ciatr.jp/topics/338893)

  - **Trompete (神の喇叭, トロンペーテ, German "trumpet", Japanese "Trumpet of God"), first used in ch. 653 "The Theatre Suicide Scene 7" [high]:** "While holding his closed fist in front of his beak as though grasping a trumpet, Lille absorbs large amounts of Reishi from the surroundings to form an **enormous golden trumpet with a wing-like appendage** in the air above him. ## 6. Personality and fight style

### Cited Findings
- He is arrogant and self-sanctified. He calls himself **an envoy of God (神の使い)** and calls opponents **sinners (罪深き者)**. A Japanese blog sums him up as 「自信過剰で傲岸不遜な『神に近い男』」(an overconfident, haughty "man closest to God") **[high]**. — [きちのうすめ雑記](https://kichitan.hatenablog.com/entry/2025/04/26/143637)
- **Rule-bound "fairness":** he keeps an eye shut because opening both would be unfair to sinners, and he treats a third opening as heresy **[high]**.
- He is a cold, observant **sniper**. He sets up at range, adjusts the dial and scope, and kills subordinates before the commander arrives **[med]**. He has superb reflexes and dodged Kageoni on the first try **[high]**. In TYBW 35 he says anyone who isn't afraid is "either a fool or a worthless fighter who deserves to be abandoned" **[med]**.
- His deadpan erudition, 「チョコラテ・イングレスか」, became a meme **[high]**. In the owl form, his composure cracks into ranting ("anime villain ranting", per a reviewer) **[med]**.

### Inferences
- **Base:** quiet, still, long pauses, a **sniper who never closes distance**. **Jilliel:** serene, floating, an untouchable angel. **Owl form:** shrieking, grotesque, bombastic. That is three very different temperaments, so the voice lines and animation tempo should change with each.

## 8. Design hooks for a 3D arena fighter (SOUL DUEL)

### Takeaway
Base: **"The Sniper"**, a long-range zoner whose **line shots ignore guard** but are **thin, telegraphed by the aim line, and lose to a sidestep**. His escape is an **eye meter (3 charges)** of phasing, and he can't block well. Awakened, Jilliel: **"The Judge"**, a floating zoner that fires **volleys from the wing holes** and **phases through the opponent's hits during a short window after each of his own attacks**. Note that this is a fairness inversion of canon. Its signature Kikon or super is **Trompete**, which **a perfect Hoho or a guard at the right moment reflects back on him**, honouring Hakkyōken. Don't copy literally: constant invincibility, instant unblockable hitscan from full screen, or a revive after K.O.

### Cited Findings (project context)
- SOUL DUEL's verbs: J/K strings, a Breaker (the grab-like dash), a guard with a **guard gauge**, Step/Hoho (iframes, reappear behind, perfect Hoho counter) on a **flash-step gauge**, Reiatsu 3 bars for SP1/SP2, the Kikon (O) rush, and one awakening per match lasting to the end. There are form-specific U precedents (Yamamoto West ward, Kenpachi DRINK). Chip applies to projectiles and pierces, and chip never kills. A blocked projectile has no attacker recovery to measure. Turning to face is capped (Signature/SP 60°/s, Breaker 120°/s), so a sideways Step beats a dash from 3 m or more. — docs/duel/DUEL_DESIGN.md §2–§4, §6
- Canon facts that become mechanics: the line pierce (§2), the three eye-openings that unlock the Vollständig (§2), constant phase in Jilliel (§3), the 24 wing muzzles (§3), the reflect defeat (§4), and Kageoni being dodged on the first try (§2). Senjumaru's anime mirror cloth was aimed at Lille (senjumaru.md).

### Inferences (design proposals, not canon)
**Base form, "万物貫通 THE X-AXIS":**
- **X-AXIS SHOT (SP1 or signature):** a **line** hit, effectively instant once fired, through **projectiles and guard**: a blocked hit still lands as heavy chip plus a guard-gauge drain, not full damage. The fairness comes from a **visible aim line** (a thin ink crosshair line on the floor) for about 20–30 f before firing, a **turn-rate lock** once aiming starts (it fits the existing 60°/s rule), and **thinness**, so **a Step to either side always dodges it**. That is the 3D-arena counter to a 2D unblockable. Long recovery when it whiffs.
- **Scope stance (U = AIM):** holding it narrows the line and adds range or damage (the dial and scope from ch. 644), but he can't move. It is the base-form analogue of Yamamoto's ward.
- **EYE (the canon rule as a meter):** **3 pips**, shown as the crosshair eye on the HUD. Spending a pip, for example on a guard input at the moment of being hit, **phases him through one hit** (a short iframe window, like a Hoho without displacement). This is the "opening the left eye in a crisis" from ch. 645. **The third pip spent unlocks or charges the awakening.** That is canon's Vollständig trigger and fits the slower awakening gauge as an *extra* source, not a replacement. Pips don't refill (or refill only per round), so canon's scarcity holds.
- **Weak close range:** no strong melee in canon. J/K are rifle-butt and bayonet-style short strings with poor reach. He wins by **keeping distance**, and the opponent's Breaker or dash-in is his main threat.
- **Kageoni reflex (optional flavour):** an anti-ground "hop" Step with good iframes against low or shadow attacks (the first-try dodge).

**Awakened, 「神の裁き」JILLIEL:**
- The awakening cinematic: the left eye opens for the third time, the crosshair mark flares, and wings unfold from the cocoon robe as the halo appears. His silhouette loses its arms. He **hovers**, but the camera and hit volumes stay grounded. Flight is visual only, since SOUL DUEL has no jump.
- **WING VOLLEY:** pressing the signature fires **3 to 8 thin piercing shots from the wing muzzles in a fan or in sequence**. It is zoning that **covers sidestep angles**, the counter to the base form's weakness, but each shot is weaker than the base X-AXIS SHOT.
- **Phase (the canon constant intangibility, made fair):** canon says he is untouchable all the time. Literally that is unwinnable and unfun. Two options:
  - (a) **phase only in the X frames after he fires** (he "rides" his own X-Axis), so he is **hittable while charging**;
  - (b) **phase drains Reiatsu per hit absorbed**, so pressure empties it.
  Recommend **(a) inverted**: he is **solid while firing** and **phases while moving**. That rewards hitting him *as he commits to a shot*, which is readable. Label it a SOUL DUEL rule; canon has no "solid while attacking" exception.
- **Status counters are canon-flavoured:** DOT, drain or "shared damage" effects (Kyōraku's Bankai logic) and **reflects** ignore his phase. Grabs or Breakers could count as "rules" that ignore it too. That decision belongs to the user.
- **TROMPETE (Kikon or SP2 finisher):** he mimes a trumpet at his mouth, a golden trumpet with a wing forms overhead, a long and loud wind-up, then a **huge line or cone blast**. Its counterplay is the canon defeat: **a perfect guard or perfect Hoho during the blast reflects part of it back onto him and breaks his halo**, which ends the awakening early or removes the phase for the rest of the match. That gives a high-skill answer to his biggest move and is faithful to Nanao's Hakkyōken.

**Owl second form ("true form"): treat as optional:**
- A literal **revive after K.O.** breaks the 1v1 round rules. The alternatives:
  - (1) a **cinematic only**, where the Trompete Kikon's art shows the owl head;
  - (2) a **"last stand" phase**: below X% HP while awakened he turns golden and owl-headed, gains Sabaki no Kōmyō (golden chopping light waves) and loses the phase, trading defence for offence.
  Option (2) fits SOUL DUEL's rule that an awakening changes playstyle, not power.

**What would be unfun if copied literally:**
- Instant, full-screen, unblockable and undodgeable hitscan. Any guard-ignoring line needs a telegraph and a sidestep answer.
- Constant intangibility in Jilliel. Nothing could interact with it except a few "rule" moves.
- Regeneration or revival after beheading. It erases the K.O.
- Flight that puts him out of reach of melee. Keep the hurtbox reachable at ground height.
- A pure keep-away CPU: the AI v2 reflexes need a distance-keeping step with limits, otherwise matches stall past the 210 s gate window.

### Gaps
- No canon numbers (range, startup, beam width) exist for any of this. All tuning is design work.
- Whether grabs and Breakers ignore the Jilliel phase is a balance decision for the user.
- Whether to depict the bird-remnant ending at all. It is canon but anticlimactic, so it is probably best left out.
