# Sidegrade transformations and awakenings in fighting games: patterns, examples and pitfalls for SOUL DUEL

Scope: transformations, awakenings and mode switches that change *how* a character plays rather than *how strong* it is; how anime fighters encode canon costs; how zoning and trap characters stay fair in 3D. Context: SOUL DUEL (docs/duel/DUEL_DESIGN.md). Its awakening is once per match and permanent. Yamamoto's Bankai already switches between East and West stances: East deals the pierce and takes ×1.5 damage; West is a 360° ward without blockstun that never refills and drops on any attack command. Kenpachi's Nozarashi climbs a three-cup NOME ladder with its own drain. User rule: a new character's awakened form must differ in playstyle, not in power.

Research budget note: about 25 search and fetch calls. Several wiki pages (Naruto Storm fandom) returned HTTP 402. Claims I could not source are in Inferences or Gaps.

---

## Q1. Which concrete mechanisms make both the awakened and the base form viable?

### Takeaway
Rebirth of Souls itself does **not** make its awakening a sidegrade. It is an explicit power ramp: roughly +20 % damage, +1 Konpaku per Kikon, then further stages. The working sidegrade patterns come from other games. They share five properties. The two forms (1) spend or trade **the same scarce resource**, (2) lose named tools in exchange for new ones, (3) can switch back, or pay to stay, (4) have value that changes by matchup and situation, and (5) put the character in a **fragile state** when the resource runs out. Asuka R (GGST), Zato-1/Eddie (GGST), Carl/Nirvana (BlazBlue) and Susano'o (BBCF) are the clearest working examples.

### Cited Findings

**Bleach: Rebirth of Souls (RoS), the reference game, where awakening is a ramp:**
- The Fighting Spirit gauge fills when you deal *or* take damage. Once it is full, "EVOLUTION" appears. Awakening grants "Quick and Flash Attacks gaining almost a 20 percent damage boost". Awakened Kikon moves remove an extra Konpaku (3, or 4 with Soul Break). "Several characters will gain additional mechanics on top of their base attacks or unlock new moves altogether." — [TheGamer, Awakening Explained](https://www.thegamer.com/bleach-rebirth-of-souls-awakening-explained/)
- Stages after awakening: the gauge keeps charging. Filling it the first time auto-enters **Kikon Channel**, which unlocks a second awakened Kikon move (4 Konpaku, 5 with Soul Break). Charging Kikon Channel leads to **Spirit Drive**, which enhances attacks and movement speed. — [TheGamer](https://www.thegamer.com/bleach-rebirth-of-souls-awakening-explained/); the search summary of the same article also appears in [Bandai Namco EU Starter Guide](https://en.bandainamcoent.eu/bleach/news/bleach-rebirth-of-souls-starter-guide)
- **Reawakening** replaces Spirit Drive for Ulquiorra, Ichigo (Bankai), Aizen and Uryu. It is triggered manually and may have conditions. For example, Aizen must lose all his Konpaku, and his Fourth Fusion requires raising Fighting Spirit four times. Ichigo (Bankai) must lose all Konpaku with Fighting Spirit maxed during Sublimation; he returns as Full Hollow Ichigo with two Konpaku bars. Ulquiorra (Segunda Etapa) and Uryu (Letzt Stil) use L3+R3 after maxing the meter during Sublimation. Reawakened "basic and special attacks get an upgrade and are far more lethal". — [Destructoid Reawakening guide](https://www.destructoid.com/bleach-rebirth-of-souls-reawakening-guide-all-characters/); [TheGamer](https://www.thegamer.com/bleach-rebirth-of-souls-awakening-explained/)
- **Exceptions, the closest RoS gets to a sidegrade:** Final Getsugatensho (Dangai) Ichigo **has no Awakening at all**. His Signature moves are unguardable. His first two Kikon activations deal **no Konpaku damage** and only buff him; the third always triggers Mugetsu and kills in one hit. — [TheGamer Ichigo guide via search](https://www.thegamer.com/bleach-rebirth-of-souls-ichigo-kurosaki-character-guide/); [TheGamer Awakening](https://www.thegamer.com/bleach-rebirth-of-souls-awakening-explained/). Aizen must fill the gauge **twice** before he can awaken. — [TheGamer](https://www.thegamer.com/bleach-rebirth-of-souls-awakening-explained/)
- Ichigo is split into **three separate roster slots**: Shikai → awakens to Bankai; Bankai (own slot) → awakens to Hollowfication, "a boost in Spiritual Power" so moves deal more damage, then Reawakens to Full Hollow; Final Getsugatensho (own slot, no awakening). — [Xbox Wire, three Ichigos](https://news.xbox.com/en-us/2025/03/17/bleach-rebirth-of-souls-ichigo/); [TheGamer Ichigo guide](https://www.thegamer.com/bleach-rebirth-of-souls-ichigo-kurosaki-character-guide/)
- Kenpachi's RoS awakening is "simply taking off his eyepatch, which serves as a power limiter". It is a pure power-up, faithful to canon. — [TV Tropes / TheGamer via search](https://tvtropes.org/pmwiki/pmwiki.php/VideoGame/BleachRebirthOfSouls). (SOUL DUEL deliberately diverges: Nozarashi, no eyepatch, per DUEL_DESIGN.md §1.)
- **Conflict:** TheGamer says awakening is "not permanent — it's a temporary state that continues advancing through subsequent phases" ([TheGamer](https://www.thegamer.com/bleach-rebirth-of-souls-awakening-explained/)). SOUL DUEL's own mapping treats the RoS awakening as lasting to the end of the match (DUEL_DESIGN.md §1). The article's wording may mean it *keeps progressing* rather than that it expires. Unresolved.

**Games where the mode is a real sidegrade (both states viable):**
- **Asuka R (GGST): deck swap plus mana as both ammunition and armour.** Four spell slots are drawn from a 30-card deck. There are three "Test Cases": 1 zoning, 2 mixups, 3 unusual or checkmate effects. Mana starts at 100 %. Attacking spells typically cost 16 % and buffs 4 %, and getting hit drains it. **While he has mana, a mana shield makes him "by far the tankiest character". At 0 the shield breaks until mana is full again, making him "by far the frailest", "with even simple combos melting his lifebar."** — [EventHubs Asuka guide](https://www.eventhubs.com/news/2023/may/19/guide-asuka-guilty-gear-strive/); [Dustloop Asuka R](https://www.dustloop.com/w/GGST/Asuka_R) (Spells/Counterstrategy pages linked there). This is the cleanest "spending offence costs defence" coupling in a shipped game.
- **Zato-1/Eddie (GGST): a summoned second body on a health-bar gauge.** The Eddie gauge (3000 HP) drains 3 HP/frame while Eddie is idle, and each Eddie attack costs gauge. If Zato **unsummons voluntarily**, it refills fast (55 HP/frame). If **Zato or Eddie is hit, or the gauge empties**, it enters red "penalty recovery" (8 HP/frame, 375 f), with no resummon until it is full. — [Dustloop GGST Zato-1 (search summary)](https://www.dustloop.com/w/GGST/Zato-1). The design rewards *voluntarily leaving* the strong state and punishes being *forced* out.
- **Carl Clover/Nirvana (BlazBlue):** the same structure as Eddie. Nirvana's health is spent by attacking *and* by being hit. It regenerates fast if she is rested, but if it runs out she is disabled and regenerates slowly until full. "Carl lives and dies by Nirvana's gauge." — [Dustloop BBCF Carl](https://www.dustloop.com/w/BBCF/Carl_Clover)
- **Susano'o (BBCF): a seal ramp inside a round.** He has eight specials but starts each round with only one unlocked. His Drive unlocks them by connecting (hit **or block**) while a cycling highlight is on the seal. Overdrive temporarily grants all specials, and a DD usable only in Overdrive unlocks all seals. — [Dustloop Susano'o](https://www.dustloop.com/w/Susano'o); [BBCF Susano'o](https://www.dustloop.com/wiki/index.php?title=BBCF%2FSusano%27o). Canon ("sealed god") is encoded as *missing* tools, not as extra damage.
- **Melty Blood Actress Again moon styles: sidegrades chosen before the match.** Crescent is speed (the "original" version), Half is simple (weaker but combo-heavy), and Full is power, which **loses the button-press reset** and gets stronger specials. — [Wikipedia Melty Blood / TYPE-MOON wiki via search](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Melty_Blood). Type Lumina later replaced styles with Moon Drive, a timed, resource-spent boost. — [Sportskeeda MBTL system](https://www.sportskeeda.com/esports/melty-blood-type-lumina-new-battle-system)
- **SF6 Jamie: a ramp, shown for contrast.** Drink levels 0–4 unlock specials (Lv1 dive kick, Lv2 Bakkai, Lv3 Tenshin command grab, Lv4 Swagger Hermit Punch). Drinking at max refills half a Drive bar instead. Levels are gained by the drink special, forward throw, Freeflow Kicks or certain target combos. — [SuperCombo Jamie](https://wiki.supercombo.gg/w/Street_Fighter_6/Jamie). This is a pure ramp that is balanced by making **level 0 deliberately weak** and the *act of gaining levels* costly (a drink animation, needing hits). It is the same family as Kenpachi's cups.
- **Tekken 8 Heat: a universal, reversible-once-per-round, timed state.** Harada frames Heat as encouraging aggression and "dramatic changes" modelled on boxing/MMA counter punches. It can be triggered directly or by landing specific moves. — [Shacknews interview](https://www.shacknews.com/article/134835/tekken-8-katsuhiro-harada-michael-murray-interview); [Bandai Namco EU interview](https://en.bandainamcoent.eu/tekken/news/tekken-8-interview-katsuhiro-harada-and-michael-murray); [GamerBraves](https://www.gamerbraves.com/interview-katsuhiro-harada-talks-about-bringing-the-heat-system-in-tekken-8/)
- **Sparking! Zero transformations cost Skill Count, and tier skipping is allowed.** Costs vary (Goku SSJ 1, SSB 3, Vegito Blue fusion 5). Cycling through intermediate forms gives no benefit because the total cost is the same. Fusion is **permanent** for the battle, while transformations can revert to base at will. Skill Count recharges passively and from damage dealt or taken. — [GameRant](https://gamerant.com/dragon-ball-sparking-zero-transform-super-saiyan-fusion-chase-change/); [Game8](https://game8.co/games/Dragon-Ball-Sparking-Zero/archives/481316); [TheGamer S0 guide](https://www.thegamer.com/dragon-ball-sparking-zero-transformation-and-fusion-how-to-guide/) ("It can't be undone in battle"). One search summary claims that starting in base form and transforming mid-fight yields a damage bonus over picking the final form outright. I could **not verify** this on the fetched page (see Gaps).
- **Persona 4 Arena Awakening: automatic below ~35 % HP.** Damage taken is multiplied by 0.625, SP gets +50, and Awakened SP skills unlock. — [Dustloop P4AU Damage (via search)](https://www.dustloop.com/w/P4AU/Damage). A universal comeback buff, *not* a sidegrade.

### Inferences
- The working sidegrades pay for their strong mode with the **same resource that protects them** (Asuka's mana = shield; Eddie/Nirvana's HP = availability; West's guard gauge = ward). The choice to spend is automatically a choice to become vulnerable. That tension makes "when to switch" a decision rather than an obvious yes.
- "Voluntary exit is cheap, forced exit is expensive" (Eddie's white vs red recovery) is a transferable rule for SOUL DUEL-style stance switches. West already has part of it: a Breaker or crush drops him to East. What it lacks is a reward for leaving *voluntarily*.
- The canon-faithful sidegrades encode their theme as **lost tools** (Susano'o's seals, Full Moon losing the reset, Dangai Ichigo losing awakening but gaining unguardable Signatures). Tuning a single multiplier does not have this effect.

### Gaps
- No primary Bandai Namco/Tamsoft developer interview found on awakening *intent* (sidegrade vs power).
- Exact frame and damage tables for RoS awakened kits were not fetched. The community wiki for RoS appears thin.
- Dissidia (EX Mode / Brave-HP), Granblue Versus (character-specific modes such as Zeta/Vaseraga), JJK Cursed Clash domain in-game rules, and UNI Vorpal detail were not covered in depth. UNI Vorpal: the player with more GRD at cycle end gets Vorpal, a temporary attack boost, and Celestial Vorpal when GRD > 6. — [Mizuumi UNI System](https://mizuumi.wiki/w/Under_Night_In-Birth/System) (via search only).

## Q2. Typical failure modes (awakening strictly better, awakening a trap, snowball) and their fixes

### Takeaway
RoS shows the two classic failures at once. Awakening is strictly better (a multiplier plus more Konpaku per Kikon, stacking into Kikon Channel and Spirit Drive), and its charge rule (fill on damage *taken*) turns it into a comeback-for-the-loser that competitive players call punishing skill. Bandai's fixes were blunt: slower gauge gain and making awakened attacks uncounterable, instead of adding trade-offs. MvC3 Dark Phoenix shows the fix for a strictly-better form, which is a built-in HP drain, and also the failure when the gate (5 bars) is paired with a huge payoff: the character became a feast-or-famine lock.

### Cited Findings
- **Awakening as comeback, criticised as anti-skill:** a Steam user wrote "If you take your opponents Kikon first, they will get awakening and sublimation faster than you. Meaning the game has a built in comeback mechanic for you being GOOD at the game." — [Steam discussion (via search)](https://steamcommunity.com/app/1689620/discussions/0/597396334368052762). Reviewers were split. Shacknews-style praise: "If you're getting hammered, you'll usually reach that awakening faster than your foe… a fun, almost cinematic quality". A review contrasts it with Tekken 8 Heat because "rather than acting as a quick reversal… it instead adds a new layer to combat mid-battle that complicates the situation for both players". — [search summary of Shacknews / TheSixthAxis / PushSquare reviews](https://www.shacknews.com/article/143717/bleach-rebirth-of-souls-review-score); [TheSixthAxis](https://www.thesixthaxis.com/2025/03/31/bleach-rebirth-of-souls-review/)
- PushSquare: mechanics "feel unrefined and poorly balanced"; "very little room for things like combo expression or more advanced defensive techniques, because repetitive three-button strings and the simple act of blocking negate so much". — [PushSquare review](https://www.pushsquare.com/reviews/ps5/bleach-rebirth-of-souls) (relevant to SOUL DUEL's contact-gated J/K strings and guard gauge, which already address "blocking negates everything")
- **Bandai Namco patch 1.40 (3 Dec 2025):** "Fighting Spirit adjustments make immediate Kikon Channel harder post-Awakening, reducing sudden match-ending scenarios"; "Awakening/Reawakening attacks cannot be countered"; Breaker hitbox reduced because "the cost to close in and pressure an opponent using Breaker varied greatly between characters"; Kikon moves outside Soul Break restore the Reverse gauge; Reishi Deficiency increases damage output and guard chip. — [Bandai Namco America v1.40](https://www.bandainamcoent.com/news/bleach-rebirth-of-souls-version-1-40-updates); [Bandai Namco EU v1.40](https://en.bandainamcoent.eu/bleach/news/bleach-rebirth-of-souls-version-140-updates)
- 1.40 per-character changes are mostly **awakened-form cancel routes** rather than numbers. Rukia (Awakening): Quick combo and Flash can cancel into Signature; Signature recovery up, scaling improved. Kenpachi (Awakening): Signature can Step-cancel during charge; fewer frames to max charge; SP2 hitstun up, recovery down. Byakuya (Awakening): multiple Quick/Flash/Signature cancel routes. Yamamoto (Awakening): Special Flash damage up but startup slower. Ichigo Bankai (Reawakening): Special Flash travels further. Final Getsuga: Special Flash fully invincible at the start. Ichigo (Initial): Signature startup up. — [Bandai Namco America v1.40](https://www.bandainamcoent.com/news/bleach-rebirth-of-souls-version-1-40-updates)
- Community patch ("Bros Community Patch") raised the Fighting Spirit needed to awaken by 20 %, "bringing Nel's Awakening timing in line with the rest of the roster". — [eclipseworks.org (via search)](https://eclipseworks.org/). This is a fan mod, not official.
- **MvC3 Dark Phoenix:** with 5 bars when Phoenix's HP empties, she revives as Dark Phoenix with melee and ranged "far, far more powerful". Her HP **drains on its own** unless X-Factor is active, "forcing players to bring up a healing field if their opponents managed to survive her initial onslaught". — [EventHubs Dark Phoenix retrospective](https://www.eventhubs.com/news/2022/may/15/marvel-capcom-3-dark-phoenix/); [MvC fandom Phoenix/Gameplay](https://marvelvscapcom.fandom.com/wiki/Phoenix/Gameplay). The UMVC3 community developed dedicated "anti-Dark Phoenix tech" ahead of Evo 2023. — [EventHubs 2023](https://www.eventhubs.com/news/2023/mar/08/anti-dark-phoenix-tech-umvc3/). Base Phoenix was widely seen as weak. — [Steam UMVC3 "Why is Phoenix so weak?"](https://steamcommunity.com/app/357190/discussions/0/133261124631645011/)
- **Asuka's fix for a snowballing shield:** the shield breaks at 0 and **stays broken until mana is back to full**, a hysteresis like SOUL DUEL's "guardless until full". — [EventHubs](https://www.eventhubs.com/news/2023/may/19/guide-asuka-guilty-gear-strive/)
- **Sparking Zero's fix for "just rush the top form":** a cost per tier with the same total whether or not intermediate forms are skipped, and fusion made irreversible. — [GameRant](https://gamerant.com/dragon-ball-sparking-zero-transform-super-saiyan-fusion-chase-change/)

### Inferences (failure modes → fixes, as a table for the report writer)

| Failure mode | Seen in | Fix pattern (source-backed where cited above) |
|---|---|---|
| Awakening strictly better (multiplier + more Kikon) → "always awaken on sight" | RoS base system | Give the awakened form a **standing liability** (Bankai East's ×1.5 damage taken in SOUL DUEL already; Dark Phoenix drain; Asuka frail at 0) and **remove** named base tools. |
| Comeback gauge fills from damage *taken* → rewards the losing player, frustrates the winner | RoS (Steam criticism) | Fill mainly from **own contact** (SOUL DUEL's KŌSEI pays only own hit windows); or make the gauge **a choice**, not an automatic comeback |
| Stacking tiers → sudden match end | RoS Kikon Channel → Spirit Drive (Bandai nerfed gain in 1.40) | Cap the count per event (SOUL DUEL already caps 4 Konpaku per event); one awakening, no further tiers |
| Gate too high, payoff too high → feast or famine; the base form is a husk | MvC3 Phoenix | Keep the base form a complete character; make the gate cheaper and the payoff narrower |
| Transformation is a trap (costs more than it returns) | Implied by "cycling gives no benefit" in S0; Asuka at 0 mana | Voluntary exit refunds (Eddie's fast white recovery), so trying the mode isn't a permanent loss |
| Permanent sidegrade is chosen by matchup alone → effectively character select | Melty moons (chosen pre-match) | If the mode is permanent, the choice must happen **mid-match with information** (e.g. awaken into A or B), or be reversible |
| Snowball: the strong mode also builds the resource that sustains it | Heat/Tension-like loops | Separate the **paying** meter from the **earning** meter, or add a constant drain (Nozarashi cup 3's 10/s) |

- Bandai's own 1.40 response (slower gain, uncounterable awakened attacks, cancel routes) *increased* awakened power in places. The official direction for RoS is therefore "awakening = climax", not "awakening = sidegrade". SOUL DUEL's user rule is a deliberate departure, and RoS patches will not provide precedents for it.

### Gaps
- No official Bandai statement of design philosophy on whether awakening should be optional or strictly better.
- Reddit threads specifically on "forced reawakening" (e.g. Aizen's condition of losing all Konpaku) were not retrieved; only the conditions themselves are sourced.
- Patches 1.10–1.30 and post-1.40 (2026) notes were not fetched. Whether later 2026 patches changed awakening further is unknown.

## Q3. How faithful anime fighters encode signature costs and limitations of transformations

### Takeaway
The best encodings translate the canon limitation into a **mechanical asymmetry the opponent can exploit**, not into flavour text or a small number. Examples: a time limit becomes a drain (Dark Phoenix, Nozarashi cup 3), a seal becomes locked moves (Susano'o), "one misstep kills" becomes a fragile state (Asuka at 0 mana), a one-shot ultimate becomes a counter (Dangai Ichigo's Mugetsu on the 3rd Kikon), and irreversibility becomes irreversibility (S0 fusion). RoS mostly encodes canon as *power* (Kenpachi's eyepatch = limiter removed), which is faithful but not a sidegrade.

### Cited Findings
- **Rukia, Hakka no Togame (canon):** her body goes to absolute zero, touching her freezes the toucher, she must release it slowly to avoid injury, and she "can only safely maintain the temperature of absolute zero for four seconds"; Byakuya warns "a single misstep could end Rukia's life". — [Bleach Wiki (Fandom) Rukia](https://bleach.fandom.com/wiki/Rukia_Kuchiki); [Fiction Horizon](https://fictionhorizon.com/bleach-rukias-bankai-hakka-no-togame-explained/). This maps naturally to **a short, timed state with a self-freeze recovery if held too long** (the "absolute-zero self-freeze" drawback in the brief). In RoS, Rukia's awakening is treated as buffs and cancel routes (1.40: Quick/Flash → Signature cancels). — [Bandai 1.40](https://www.bandainamcoent.com/news/bleach-rebirth-of-souls-version-1-40-updates). No evidence that RoS models the 4-second limit.
- **Dangai (Final Getsuga) Ichigo:** no awakening; unguardable Signatures; the Kikon is a self-buff for the first two uses and Mugetsu (instant kill) on the third. — [TheGamer Ichigo guide (via search)](https://www.thegamer.com/bleach-rebirth-of-souls-ichigo-kurosaki-character-guide/). Canon's "last power, spends everything" becomes a **countdown the opponent can see and play around**.
- **Aizen:** needs Fighting Spirit twice to awaken; Reawakening (Fourth Fusion) requires losing all Konpaku; Kyōka Suigetsu can nullify Konpaku damage. — [TheGamer](https://www.thegamer.com/bleach-rebirth-of-souls-awakening-explained/); [Destructoid](https://www.destructoid.com/bleach-rebirth-of-souls-reawakening-guide-all-characters/). Canon's "evolves by being pushed" is encoded as a condition you must survive into.
- **Kenpachi (RoS):** the eyepatch as limiter = a pure power-up. — [TV Tropes (via search)](https://tvtropes.org/pmwiki/pmwiki.php/VideoGame/BleachRebirthOfSouls)
- **Susano'o:** canon "sealed" → locked specials unlocked by landing hits. — [Dustloop](https://www.dustloop.com/w/Susano'o)
- **Dark Phoenix:** canon "cosmic force consumes the host" → HP drain. — [EventHubs](https://www.eventhubs.com/news/2022/may/15/marvel-capcom-3-dark-phoenix/)
- **JJK Domain Expansion (canon rules a game can encode):** a sure-hit barrier; counters are Domain Clash (the more refined domain wins over time), Simple Domain (nullifies sure-hit around the user), and Hollow Wicker Basket. — [GameRant JJK domain counters](https://gamerant.com/jujutsu-kaisen-counters-domain-expansions/); [JJK Wiki Domain Expansion](https://jujutsu-kaisen.fandom.com/wiki/Domain_Expansion). Canon itself supplies the **counterplay kit**: a zone that forces hits, beaten by a small personal bubble or by out-refining it.
- **Sparking Zero:** canon form hierarchy → Skill Count price per form; fusion permanent as in canon (Potara). — [GameRant](https://gamerant.com/dragon-ball-sparking-zero-transform-super-saiyan-fusion-chase-change/)
- **Naruto Storm Awakening:** triggered after losing a set amount of health and charging chakra; "the health requirement differing based on how powerful the transformation is"; grants new abilities, speed and stronger attacks. — [Naruto UNS Wiki (search summary)](https://naruto-ultimate-ninja-storm.fandom.com/wiki/Naruto:_Ultimate_Ninja_Storm). **Scaling the threshold to the power** is a simple lever: stronger awakenings require being closer to death.
- **SOUL DUEL precedents already in the repo** (DUEL_DESIGN.md §6.1–6.2): Bankai East = canon "the edge that burns everything" as pierce + ×1.5 damage taken; West = canon "garb of fire that defends" as a never-refilling 360° ward that breaks on any attack command. Nozarashi = canon "drink" (NOME) as a three-cup ladder with drains and the cup-3 DRINK guard.

### Inferences
- A clean encoding recipe: **canon limit → the opponent's counterplay verb**. "4 seconds at absolute zero" → the opponent's verb is *wait it out / bait the overheld state*. "Must release slowly" → *punish the exit*. "Touching her freezes you" → *don't strike her in the state; use ranged or Breaker*. Every canon cost should name what the opponent does about it.
- Where canon has no cost (Kenpachi's limiter), a sidegrade must *invent* the trade from canon character traits (Kenpachi loves a fight → a form that is worse at safe play and better at trading). SOUL DUEL already did this: cup 3's DRINK takes half and pays with guard.

### Gaps
- No direct source on how Naruto Storm games balance specific awakenings (e.g. awakened characters losing substitution/items, Lee's Gates self-damage). The wiki page was paywalled (402), so this is unverified.
- JJK Cursed Clash's actual in-game domain rules (duration, clash resolution) were not found; only canon rules are sourced.

## Q4. Recommendations transferable to a small 1v1 3D fighter (including zoning/trap fairness vs sidestep, and dual-wield rushdown vs spacing sidegrades)

### Takeaway
For SOUL DUEL's new characters: make the awakening a **choice of trade**, not a threshold of power. Pay for new tools with removed base tools and a visible liability. Keep the damage multiplier at ≤ ×1.0 net. Use one resource that is both fuel and protection. Refund voluntary exits and punish forced ones. In 3D, zoning and traps must respect the sidestep: projectiles need either homing/width *or* long recovery, and traps need visible tells and a bounded lifetime.

### Cited Findings
- **3D fundamentals:** homing moves "keep your opponent from sidestepping or sidewalking"; moves with homing or good width beat sidestep. — [LearnTekken basic techniques](https://ampersanders.github.io/LearnTekken/basic_tech.html). A community view in the same source set: zoning "doesn't really work all that well in 3D games", and "Tekken is a game about forcing your opponent to make a mistake" via movement and whiff punishing. — [search summary citing LearnTekken / Steam threads](https://ampersanders.github.io/LearnTekken/basic_tech.html) (opinion, not developer statement).
- **Summon/trap gauges that stay fair:** Eddie drains while idle (3 HP/f) and locks out for 375 f if hit ([Dustloop Zato](https://www.dustloop.com/w/GGST/Zato-1)). Nirvana is disabled at 0 and regenerates slowly ([Dustloop Carl](https://www.dustloop.com/w/BBCF/Carl_Clover)). Nirvana "can control completely different parts of the screen… threatening mix-ups and unblockables". This is exactly why its gauge must be finite. — [Dustloop Carl](https://www.dustloop.com/w/BBCF/Carl_Clover)
- **GGST Testament** was reworked away from invisible traps to a **Stain** state applied on contact that causes an automatic follow-up. — [Guilty Gear Wiki Testament (via search)](https://guiltygear.fandom.com/wiki/Testament). Arc System Works moved from hidden setplay to a **visible marked state**.
- **Asuka Test Case split** (zoning deck vs mixup deck vs checkmate deck): one character, three playstyles, swapped mid-match, all fed by the same mana/shield. — [EventHubs](https://www.eventhubs.com/news/2023/may/19/guide-asuka-guilty-gear-strive/)
- **Tekken 8 Heat** is time-limited, once per round, and designed as an aggression tool ([Shacknews](https://www.shacknews.com/article/134835/tekken-8-katsuhiro-harada-michael-murray-interview)). This is the 3D-native precedent for a **short, reversible power window** versus RoS's permanent awakening.
- SOUL DUEL constraints in play (DUEL_DESIGN.md): one awakening per match, permanent; Kikon counts 2 → 3 when awakened (§2); KŌSEI pays Reiatsu/flash-step only for own melee contact, not `:ranged` windows, fire, lines, the cone or the rift (§4); chip never kills; guard gauge with GUARD HOLD (no refill while guarding); Bankai West already implements a "defensive sidegrade" (ward, no blockstun, never refills, drops on any attack).

### Inferences: concrete recommendations for SOUL DUEL's new characters

**A. Rules for making awakening a sidegrade (checklist for each new character)**
1. **Net power ≈ 0.** Any awakened damage-dealt multiplier > 1 must be offset by damage taken > 1, a drain, or lost tools. (East already does ×1.0+pierce / ×1.5 taken.) Consider **not** raising the Kikon count from 2 to 3 for a new character's awakening, or raising it only in exchange for a real liability. The universal "awakened Kikon = 3" rule is the RoS ramp that makes awakening strictly better.
2. **Remove at least one signature base tool** and name it in the HUD/design doc (Susano'o-style locks; Full Moon's lost reset). A base-form matchup where the lost tool matters should exist, so a good player sometimes delays awakening. Delaying is only possible if awakening isn't forced: keep it manual (SOUL DUEL already does: P from idle/walk/guard).
3. **One resource = fuel + armour** (Asuka). Spending the awakened form's special resource should weaken its defence, e.g. a form whose guard gauge *is* its ammunition.
4. **Voluntary exit cheap, forced exit expensive** (Eddie white vs red). If the new form is a stance (like East/West), leaving by choice should keep the resource; being broken out (Breaker, crush) should lock the stance for a visible time.
5. **Matchup dependence by design:** pair the base and awakened kits as *rushdown vs spacing*. For a dual-wield character: base = one blade, long reach, spacing, whiff punishing; awakened = two blades, short reach, fast plus-frames and tracking, but loses the long poke and takes a liability (e.g. no guard while dual-wielding, only parry/step). Against a zoner, awakening is good; against a Kenpachi-style trader, the base form's reach is better. This is inference and needs a seed-gate test in SOUL DUEL's CPU-vs-CPU harness.
6. **Canon cost → opponent's counterplay verb** (Q3). For a Rukia-like ice character, Hakka no Togame could be a **held state capped at ~4 s** (canon's own number): while held, touching her freezes the attacker and her moves freeze on contact. On release or timeout she enters a long self-thaw recovery that the opponent can punish, and holding past the cap self-freezes her (a stun). Base Shikai = mid-range dance zoning (Some no mai / Tsugi no mai), awakened = a point-blank trap. Sidegrade, not ramp.
7. **Avoid gauge-from-damage-taken as the only fill.** RoS's criticism is that it rewards losing. SOUL DUEL's Fighting Spirit gives +0.07/damage taken and +15 per Konpaku lost, so it is already partly a comeback gauge. For sidegrade awakenings this matters less (no power gain means no comeback swing). **That is itself an argument for the sidegrade rule**: once awakening is not a power-up, the comeback timing stops being a balance problem.

**B. Zoning/trap/setplay in a 3D arena (SOUL DUEL has 8 m resets, Step/Hoho, 360° movement)**
1. Every projectile or trap declares its **anti-sidestep answer**: either tracking/width (cheap, short range) or a lane (long range, beaten by one Step, long recovery). Don't give both. SOUL DUEL's ENJO lane (fire walls along a lane) and KYOKUJITSUJIN's 25°/9 m cone already follow "lane or cone, not homing".
2. Traps need **a visible tell, a bounded lifetime and a finite count** (Testament's move from invisible traps to visible Stain; Eddie's idle drain). Suggested cap: at most 1–2 live set pieces, each expiring in ≤ 3–4 s, destroyed by the opponent's Breaker or by hitting the owner (Eddie's rule: hit the owner → the summon collapses and locks out).
3. **Zoning shouldn't farm the aggression economy**: SOUL DUEL's KŌSEI already pays nothing for `:ranged` windows. Keep that true for any new zoner so its awakened spacing form can't out-earn a rushdown form.
4. Chip-never-kills plus the guard gauge means a zoner's blocked projectiles are its pressure tool. Give zoner projectiles a **low guard value** (fire hazard currently 12) so the zoner can't crush guard from full screen. Crush should need melee contact.
5. Setplay after a knockdown or reset: SOUL DUEL resets both fighters to 8 m apart with 48 f of neutral where inputs are ignored. Setplay characters must not be able to pre-place traps that are live when the 48 f ends. Clear all hazards at the reset (unverified whether this is already done; check `hazards.lisp`).

**C. Dual-wield rushdown vs spacing sidegrades (pattern notes)**
- The base/awakened contrast works best on **range band + defensive option**, not damage: long-reach single blade with a guard vs short-reach dual blades with parry/step only. The trade-off is *which defensive verb you keep*, which SOUL DUEL already uses (U = guard / West ward / DRINK per form).
- Make the rushdown form's damage come from **tempo** (plus-frames, cancels, KŌSEI multiplier from low own guard) rather than multipliers, so its total is equal but the player's decisions differ.
