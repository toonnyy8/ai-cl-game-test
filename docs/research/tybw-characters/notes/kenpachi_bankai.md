# Zaraki Kenpachi's Bankai (TYBW manga + anime), for a second awakening in SOUL DUEL

Context for the reader: SOUL DUEL's Kenpachi already awakens into Shikai Nozarashi (release word 「呑め」 NOME) with a 3-cup ladder (KATATE → RYOTE → NOMIHOSE, drink/guard, rift, Kikon 2/3/4). The user has ruled that he wears **no eyepatch in any form** (docs/duel/DUEL_NOZARASHI_V2.md, DUEL_DESIGN §6.2). Bankai would be a second awakening stacked on that. Research date: 2026-09-27. Fandom wiki (bleach.fandom.com) returned HTTP 402 to the fetch tool, so the wiki itself was not read; facts below come from chapter summaries, episode reviews and news pages.

## 1. Canon facts: name, chapter, episode, appearance, effects, drawbacks

### Takeaway
The Bankai has **no name**. It first appears in manga **chapter 669 "Blade II" (刃 II)**, where Yachiru, as Nozarashi's spirit, calls it out and triggers it after Gerard Valkyrie has beaten Kenpachi down. **Chapter 670 "The Perfect Crimson"** shows the cost: his **right arm bursts open** because the Bankai put out more power than his body can hold. Nothing about the sword is the point. **Kenpachi himself changes**, into a crimson-skinned, horned oni.

### Inferences
- The brief says "the arm he lost vs Gremmy / Pernida". **Gremmy does not belong in that sentence.** The Gremmy fight is where the Shikai first appears and where he splits the meteor. The same (right, dominant) arm is lost **twice**: he tears it off himself against Pernida (ch 636), Mayuri's pod regrows it, and his own Bankai bursts it in ch 670. The sequence is lose it, regrow it, blow it up.
- The unlock needs three beats: (a) a beating so bad he nearly dies; (b) Yachiru, who is Nozarashi, saying he has not been "using her properly"; (c) the Unohana arc before it, which removed his self-limits and gave him Nozarashi's voice. The Bankai is Yachiru finally opening the tap. Kenpachi does not learn it.
- It is all strength and savagery: teeth, fist, one downward cleave. It has no ranged technique or special property of its own.

## 2. Manga vs anime (cours 1–4, as of Sept 2026)

### Takeaway
The anime adapts the Bankai in **TYBW Part 4 "The Calamity", episode 4 "THE PERFECT CRIMSON"** (overall TYBW episode **44**), aired in **August 2026**. The events follow the manga closely. The changes are in staging: Yachiru's vision is set in a recreated Kusajishi forest, Hitsugaya and Byakuya (not Ikkaku and Yumichika) react to the red skin, her voice is distorted, and the regeneration gets heavier animation. None of cours 1–3 (Parts 1–3) contain the Bankai. Part 3 "The Conflict" covers the Pernida arm loss.

### Cited Findings
- The Calamity ep 4 "THE PERFECT CRIMSON" is where the anime reveals Kenpachi's Bankai; it aired Saturday 15 Aug 2026 — [FandomWire](https://fandomwire.com/bleach-thousand-year-blood-war-the-calamity-episode-4-review/); [bankaivids on Threads (preview)](https://www.threads.com/@bankaivids/post/DcBbEu7icX9/first-look-at-bleach-tybw-episode-is-here-the-calamity-returns-with-the-perfect/). **Conflict:** The Geekiary lists episode 44 "The Perfect Crimson" as airing **22 Aug 2026** — [The Geekiary 1×44/1×45](https://thegeekiary.com/bleach-thousand-year-blood-war-1x44-and-1x45-review-the-perfect-crimson-and-defend-you/139205). This may be an international or English-streaming date. Not resolved.
- Differences reported from the Bleach Wiki episode page: in the manga, Nozarashi appears in front of Kenpachi on the rubble of Wahrwelt; the anime moves this to a **recreation of the Kusajishi forest where he first met Yachiru**. In the manga Ikkaku and Yumichika notice the red skin; in the anime **Hitsugaya and Byakuya** do. Gerard's regeneration gets more emphasis, with the body re-forming around the Quincy Cross — [search summary citing Bleach Wiki "The Perfect Crimson (episode)"](https://bleach.fandom.com/wiki/The_Perfect_Crimson_(episode)) (wiki page not fetchable, HTTP 402)
- Anime staging, according to the Naledir review:
  - Yachiru speaks in her usual cheerful voice, but when she announces Bankai it gains "a strange multiplicity" and an "eerie distortion", which the reviewer compares to the Visoreds' Hollow-mask voice effect.
  - His fighting is "insanely unpredictable" and he swings with the "force of a creature that cannot be reasoned with".
  - The reviewer calls the look a "berserker" or Viking battle-trance aesthetic.
  - Nozarashi apologises as his sword arm is torn off, mirroring what he had just done to Gerard.
  - Verdict: "easily one of the most exciting episodes of the entire series".
  - [Naledir](https://naledir.substack.com/p/review-bleach-thousand-year-blood-e27)
- The same review also says Gerard's power ends up "bifurcating Kenpachi's own arm", which conflicts with the manga, where the arm bursts from overload. The reviewer is probably mixing up the overload with Gerard's follow-up slash.
- Wakes as "a devil-like being out for blood". Other reviews note that the anime shows "Kenpachi struggling to maintain control over his devilish appearance and keep his physical body together while housing so much power before burning out". The Bankai "didn't last long, which was fair, because it didn't in the manga either". Criticism: Gerard's regeneration made him a poor showcase opponent — [The Geekiary](https://thegeekiary.com/bleach-thousand-year-blood-war-1x44-and-1x45-review-the-perfect-crimson-and-defend-you/139205)
- "The anime does a much better job of showcasing just how terrifying Kenpachi's Bankai is"; Gerard is pushed onto the back foot the moment the Bankai activates — [Anime Ignite](https://animeignite.com/bleach-tybw-the-perfect-crimson-episode-review-zaraki-bankai-reveal/)
- Other Part 4 anime-original changes noted so far involve Orihime, Ichigo's "Blood Chain" form and Yhwach vs Renji/Rukia. That article lists **no** Kenpachi-specific anime-original material — [Leisurebyte](https://leisurebyte.com/bleach-tybw-part-4-anime-vs-manga/)

### Inferences
- The anime is the fuller visual reference: pupil-less eyes, a distorted doubled voice at the call, the Kusajishi-forest vision, and a broken cleaver whose hilt looks like the first Zangetsu. Every one of those is a cue a game can use. Two of them (the forest vision and the voice distortion) are anime-only.
- The episode title matches ch 670's title ("The Perfect Crimson"). **Crimson** is the official visual keyword. It also sets the Bankai apart from the Shikai's yellow reiatsu, which fits SOUL DUEL's NOMIHOSE yellow pillar.

- The Calamity's total episode count and premiere date were not confirmed from an official source. Episode 44 = cour 4 ep 4 implies cour 3 ended at ep 40; not verified.

## 4. Design hooks for "power his body can't contain"

### Takeaway
The canon gives a ready-made structure: **near-death trigger → Yachiru opens the tap → crimson oni, pure-strength attacks that ignore size → the arm bursts after a fixed number of big strikes → he fights on as a one-armed brawler → collapse.** Make the self-destruction a **countdown the player spends**, not a timer that ticks down on its own, and take away his cleaver arm when it runs out.

### Cited Findings
- It is triggered at the low point, after being knocked out — [Naledir](https://naledir.substack.com/p/review-bleach-thousand-year-blood-e27)
- The body breaks on roughly the 4th attack: "right arm tore itself to pieces when he attacked Gerard for a fourth time" — [search summary, ch 670](https://www.fandompost.com/2016/04/21/bleach-chapter-670-manga-review/)
- Attacks are teeth, a fist and one clean top-down cleave — [DaddyJim ch 669](https://daddyjim.ai/bleach/manga-chapter/669-II)
- After the arm goes he fights on one-armed, grabbing Gerard's feet and heaving — [DaddyJim ch 671](https://daddyjim.ai/bleach/manga-chapter/671-The-Perfect-Crimson-2)
- It is short-lived: "didn't last long ... it didn't in the manga either" — [The Geekiary](https://thegeekiary.com/bleach-thousand-year-blood-war-1x44-and-1x45-review-the-perfect-crimson-and-defend-you/139205)
- The community wants a Bankai limited to the final health bar that is "not a free resurrection" — [Steam](https://steamcommunity.com/app/1689620/discussions/0/685243980153304713/)

### Inferences (design proposals, [G] = our interpretation)
- **Entry [G]**: available only from NOMIHOSE (cup 3), at or below a red-HP threshold, or as a one-time "get up" when a hit would KO him (the Yachiru vision plays as the cinematic). A one-time revive is exactly the "free resurrection" worry, so if it is used, revive at very low HP and let the opponent prevent it by ending with a Kikon or finisher.
- **"KORE WA BANKAI" meter (arm integrity) [G]**:
  - 4 pips, one per big strike, taken from the canon fourth attack.
  - Each K, SP or Sig spends a pip and deals huge damage that cuts through guard and armour.
  - Light attacks are free.
  - When the 4th pip is spent, **the right arm bursts**: Kenpachi loses the cleaver moveset and drops to a one-armed brawler set (bite command grab, the "grab the feet" throw, punches).
  - He also takes self-damage and a short stagger that the opponent can punish. That is his vulnerable moment, the slash Gerard got in.
  - This turns "power his body can't contain" into a choice the player makes about when to spend each strike.
- **Self-damage per strike [G]**: every Bankai heavy costs Kenpachi a little HP, or eats into his red life. It stacks with NOME's "bleed to climb" identity without copying it: NOME is drinking blood, Bankai is his body failing.
- **No guard at all [G]**: NOMIHOSE already replaces guarding with drinking. In Bankai, U could become nothing, or a roar that absorbs hits as super-armour paid in HP (the oni takes hits).
- **Signature moves, each tied to a canon beat**:
  - bite, a command grab that ripped Gerard's arm off, could apply ABA-style "arm cut" (the opponent's next K is disabled for a few seconds);
  - a launching punch (knocked Gerard off Wahrwelt);
  - a single vertical cleave that splits in two (the Vollständig kill);
  - the one-arm foot-grab throw after the arm goes.
- **Visuals**: skin shifts to crimson, two forehead horns, pupil-less eyes, **red** reiatsu shockwave on activation (against Shikai yellow), a broken cleaver with a Zangetsu-like hilt (anime), Yachiru's doubled/distorted voice on the Bankai call (anime-only), the arm bursting from the inside with a spray of blood.
- **Counterplay**: it is short and self-limiting, and the opponent survives by staying out of range or guarding/armouring through the 4 strikes, as Gerard did by regenerating. Give him one gap-closer (a leap) so RoS's "weak range" complaint doesn't turn the form into dead time.
- **What it should not be**: a flat damage multiplier or a timer. Per DUEL_NOZARASHI_V2 §0, the multipliers belong to Hellfire (×1.30) and East (×1.20). Bankai should be defined by the arm countdown and the moveset swap.

### Gaps
- Nothing canon says how long the Bankai can be held or whether he can use it again after ch 671. The whole design depends on the "4th strike" snippet, so verify it against the actual ch 670 pages before locking the pip count.
- The Unohana training arc's exact role in unlocking the Bankai (as opposed to the Shikai) is interpretation (Animehunch); it is not stated in canon text found here.
