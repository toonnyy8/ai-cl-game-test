# Kuchiki Rukia (TYBW), Sode no Shirayuki, and the Bankai Hakka no Togame (白霞罸): research for a SOUL DUEL character

Research date: 2026-09-27. Main sources: Bleach Wiki (fandom) page wikitext pulled through the MediaWiki API (the rendered pages sit behind Cloudflare). Every Bleach Wiki claim below carries the manga chapter and page, or the anime episode, that the wiki cites. Other sources: Oricon and ABEMA Times (Japanese press on anime ep. 19), the official RoS site, Bandai Namco EU patch notes, the Brave Souls fandom wiki, and reviews.
Episode numbering: TYBW ep. N = overall anime ep. 366 + N. So ep. 385 = TYBW 19 and ep. 411 = TYBW 45.

## 1. Canon mechanics of Hakka no Togame (白霞罸) and its limits and costs

### Takeaway
Sode no Shirayuki's true power does not project cold from the blade. It drops Rukia's own body temperature, so whatever she or the blade touches freezes. The Bankai widens that absolute-zero field into a pillar-and-wave area freeze. Canon states three costs: absolute zero can only be held safely for about 4 seconds, she has to thaw slowly or damage herself (her hand cracks), and one misstep could kill her. Early in TYBW her mobility in Bankai was badly limited. Only the 2026 anime-original fight against Yhwach shows her moving at near-normal speed.

### Cited Findings
- **The true Shikai power** (ch. 567–568, ep. 385): Sode no Shirayuki's "actual ability is to bring the body temperature of its wielder to a temperature below freezing. As a result, anything its wielder touches freezes, the sword itself merely increasing their reach." Rukia can "momentarily 'kill' her body by controlling her own Reishi": all molecular motion stops, so nothing (such as Äs Nödt's fear toxin) can get into her body. **Loophole:** an *optical* attack still works on her, because her nerves are still running (ch. 568 p13). — [Bleach Wiki: Rukia Kuchiki (wikitext)](https://bleach.fandom.com/wiki/Rukia_Kuchiki)
- **Temperature ladder, as staged in ep. 385 (following ch. 567):**
  - **−18 °C.** She Shunpo-cuts past Äs and freezes his shoulder: "blood freezes and will not flow out of any wound."
  - **−50 °C.** She freezes the water under the ground, which causes an **ice quake**.
  - **−273.15 °C.** She "can only function at absolute zero for four seconds" and freezes Äs solid.
  - Afterwards she "begins slowly raising her body temperature," notes that "it takes time to undo it," and finds "a small cut on her thumb." She reads the cut as a sign that she stayed at absolute zero for slightly more than 4 s.
  - — [Bleach Wiki: Episode 385 "The White Haze"](https://bleach.fandom.com/wiki/Episode_385); manga ch. 567 pp13–18 and ch. 568 p1 per [Bleach Wiki: Rukia Kuchiki](https://bleach.fandom.com/wiki/Rukia_Kuchiki)
- **Wind-up:** she had to *dodge* Äs's thorns at first "until her body adjusted." After that she caught a thorn bare-handed and froze it from the point of contact. The true power takes time to draw out; it is not instant. — [Bleach Wiki: Rukia Kuchiki](https://bleach.fandom.com/wiki/Rukia_Kuchiki) (ch. 567 pp1–16); [Episode 385](https://bleach.fandom.com/wiki/Episode_385)
- **Bankai special ability:** Hakka no Togame "increases the area of influence of Rukia's ability to reach the temperature of absolute zero." On release, a pillar of cold mist rises from where she stands (ch. 569 p16). Raising her blade releases "a massive wave of freezing cold (similar to Tsugi no mai, Hakuren) that freezes anything in its path to absolute zero" (ep. 385). Physical contact with her body starts freezing other people (ch. 570 pp4–5). — [Bleach Wiki: Rukia Kuchiki](https://bleach.fandom.com/wiki/Rukia_Kuchiki)
- **Official anime-site description,** quoted by Oricon on 2023-08-14:
  - 「真の能力は、所有者自身の肉体を氷点以下にするものであり、その中では全ての分子の運動は停止する」 ("its true power lowers the wielder's own body below freezing; within it all molecular motion stops")
  - 「卍解することに伴い、足元と空の両方に湖面のような凍気が発生し、中心をルキア自身を源とした凍気の柱が貫く。その際、刀の鋒の先へも凍気が走り、これに襲われた場合、全身が凍結し氷の塵となって風化する」 ("on Bankai, a lake-surface-like chill forms both underfoot and in the sky, and a pillar of cold with Rukia as its source pierces the centre; cold also runs out past the blade's tip, and anything it hits freezes entirely, weathers and crumbles into ice dust")
  - — [Oricon News 2290788](https://www.oricon.co.jp/news/2290788/full/)
- **Costs and weaknesses** (Bleach Wiki "Weaknesses" section):
  - **Temperature Stability (Shikai and Bankai):** "When raising the temperature from absolute zero, Rukia has to do so slowly or risk harming her own body" (ch. 568 p1; ch. 570 pp7–8).
  - **Mastery Difficulty:** "the slightest mistake could kill Rukia" (ch. 570 pp7–9).
  - **Time Limit:** "the amount of time she can use her Bankai appears severely limited… she must thaw slowly when leaving this state" (ch. 570 pp4–9).
  - — [Bleach Wiki: Rukia Kuchiki](https://bleach.fandom.com/wiki/Rukia_Kuchiki)
- **The first-use aftermath is the canonical picture of the cost** (ch. 570 / ep. 385):
  - A crack appears on the back of her hand.
  - Byakuya grasps her hand and his fingers crust over with ice. He tells her to undo it slowly, calls the Bankai magnificent but "perilous", and says it "could take her life with a single misstep."
  - Then comes his line that "nothing can be won by a sword swung in exchange for one's life" (the manga-summary wording: "a blade wielded with the cost of the wielder's life will not be able to protect anything").
  - — [Episode 385](https://bleach.fandom.com/wiki/Episode_385); [Rukia Kuchiki](https://bleach.fandom.com/wiki/Rukia_Kuchiki)
- **Mobility:** "her mobility in this form was initially severely limited by the sheer cold freezing and hardening her own body as well." By the penultimate fight with Yhwach (anime ep. 411) "Rukia was able to move at roughly her normal speed." — [Bleach Wiki: Rukia Kuchiki](https://bleach.fandom.com/wiki/Rukia_Kuchiki)
- **Japanese fan wikis** frame the Bankai as uniquely self-harming. ブリーチ屋敷: frozen targets shatter "like a flower dipped in liquid nitrogen", but she herself becomes so brittle that she could crumble if she moves carelessly. It also calls this the only Bankai whose activation alone risks death. — [ブリーチ屋敷: 白霞罸](https://bleachyashiki.com/hakkanotogame/)
- **Search-snippet summaries of the pixiv 百科事典 entry** (the page itself returned 403 to direct fetch) repeat the same points: 4 s at −273.15 °C, and a careful deactivation, since a mistake shatters her "like an ice statue". The claim that "exceeding 4 s is possible but immediately self-destructs" came only from the snippet, and the manga cut-on-thumb scene contradicts it: a small overstay caused a small wound, not death. — [pixiv百科: 白霞罸](https://dic.pixiv.net/a/%E7%99%BD%E9%9C%9E%E7%BD%B8) (unverified, snippet only)
- **Side effect:** activating the Bankai destroys her Ōken (Royal Guard) clothing, and she "temporarily gains new clothing" (ch. 570 p7). — [Bleach Wiki: Rukia Kuchiki](https://bleach.fandom.com/wiki/Rukia_Kuchiki)

### Inferences
- The canon "resource" is **temperature over time**, not reiatsu. It maps well to a meter that ramps −18 → −50 → −273 with a hard window at the bottom (4 s, about 240 f at 60 fps), then a mandatory slow thaw. Rushing the thaw or overstaying costs HP (the crack or cut).
- The core defensive idea is **"I am not alive right now"**: at absolute zero she is immune to anything that has to *enter* her (touch, poison, status), but not to things that act through sight or nerves. In a game this reads as immunity to contact effects and status plus heavy armour, while being unable to move or act freely.
- The Bankai is shown once in the manga as a *finishing burst*: one pillar, one wave, and the fight is over. There is no canon evidence that it works as a sustained, mobile fighting form.

### Gaps
- No named Bankai sub-techniques exist in canon. I found no source naming any technique under Hakka no Togame beyond the release itself, so the brief's "Hakka no Togame-related techniques" have no canon basis.
- There is no canon number for how long the Bankai as a whole can be held, only the ~4 s absolute-zero figure. The wiki's "Time Limit" entry is a hedged inference ("appears severely limited").
- I could not read the pixiv or ニコニコ大百科 articles directly (403 / Cloudflare), so the Japanese fan-wiki wording above is partly snippet-level.

## 2. Her TYBW Shikai toolkit and kidō

### Takeaway
The three canon dances are Some no mai: Tsukishiro (a placed circle that freezes a pillar), Tsugi no mai: Hakuren (a charged wave), and San no mai: Shirafune (an ice-blade extension). All three date from before TYBW. Her TYBW fights add the true-power body-cooling, ice walls, and freeze-on-touch. **Juhaku is anime-only, from the Arrancar arc,** and is not a TYBW technique. In TYBW canon she uses almost no kidō. Her kidō reputation, and the kidō kit Rebirth of Souls gave her, come from earlier arcs, and part of that kit is anime-filler only.

### Cited Findings
- **Release:** the command is 「舞え」 ("Dance"): 「舞え、袖白雪」. She turns the blade counter-clockwise, and blade, hilt and guard turn pure white. The guard becomes a hollow snowflake ring and a white ribbon trails from the pommel, with a rush of cold air (ch. 201 p16, ch. 264 p17). It is called "the most beautiful Zanpakutō in Soul Society" (ch. 202 p3). — [Bleach Wiki: Rukia Kuchiki](https://bleach.fandom.com/wiki/Rukia_Kuchiki)
- **初の舞・月白 Some no mai, Tsukishiro ("First Dance, Moon White"):** she draws a circle with the blade tip. Everything inside the circle, including the air above it, freezes in a pillar of light, and the target shatters shortly afterwards (ch. 201 pp17–23 / ep. 117). In TYBW-era canon she used it to nearly beat Riruka in the Fullbring arc (ch. 471). — [Bleach Wiki: Rukia Kuchiki](https://bleach.fandom.com/wiki/Rukia_Kuchiki)
- **次の舞・白漣 Tsugi no mai, Hakuren ("Next Dance, White Ripple"):** she stabs the ground once to make a large ice circle, then four more times in a semicircle in front of her. Ice particles gather at the blade tip and are released as an avalanche of cold air that flash-freezes whatever it touches (ch. 235 pp14–17 / ep. 140). The Bankai's wave is described as "similar to" Hakuren. — [Bleach Wiki: Rukia Kuchiki](https://bleach.fandom.com/wiki/Rukia_Kuchiki)
- **参の舞・白刀 San no mai, Shirafune ("Third Dance, White Sword"):** it gathers moisture into an ice blade that extends her reach. After piercing, it keeps freezing the area around the wound. She used it to kill Aaroniero after her sword was broken (ch. 268) and again at ch. 300 / ep. 197. — [Bleach Wiki: Rukia Kuchiki](https://bleach.fandom.com/wiki/Rukia_Kuchiki)
- **Ice-Rope Connection (unnamed):** an ice tether from her hand to the dropped sword lets her use the Shikai at a distance (ch. 471 / ep. 364). — [Bleach Wiki: Rukia Kuchiki](https://bleach.fandom.com/wiki/Rukia_Kuchiki)
- **General Shikai freezing:** she can freeze targets at range, make ice paths or platforms in midair (ch. 235, ch. 317), and freeze and shatter Quincy Reishi weapons (ch. 567 p7). In TYBW she freezes Bazz-B's arm when he tries to push through (ch. 586 pp3–6). — [Bleach Wiki: Rukia Kuchiki](https://bleach.fandom.com/wiki/Rukia_Kuchiki)
- **TYBW vs Äs Nödt, before the Bankai** (ep. 385 / ch. 566–568): she blocks thorns with an **ice wall**, which fails because the fear toxin seeps through the ice. After the true power adjusts, she catches thorns **bare-handed** and they freeze on contact. Then comes the Shunpo cut at −18 °C, the ice quake at −50 °C, and the absolute-zero freeze. — [Bleach Wiki: Episode 385](https://bleach.fandom.com/wiki/Episode_385)
- **樹白 Juhaku ("Tree White"): ANIME-ONLY.** She stabs the ground and a trail of ice runs to the target, freezing it "from the base up" into brittle ice (ep. 272, against Rudbornn, Arrancar arc). "Juhaku does not appear in the Manga." In the manga the matching scene (ch. 352 p1) shows the aftermath only. — [Bleach Wiki: Juhaku](https://bleach.fandom.com/wiki/Juhaku)
- **Kidō profile:**

  - She combines several spells at once, but needs incantations when chaining high-level spells (ch. 266 pp14–17 / ep. 155).

### Inferences
- A faithful TYBW moveset draws its dances from the pre-TYBW canon, since TYBW itself shows only walls, touch-freeze and the temperature ladder. Juhaku is usable as a ground-trail move, but it should be labelled anime-original.
- A kidō-heavy base kit is justified by her canon reputation ("greatest strength is her knowledge and skill in Kidō"), even though she barely casts in TYBW.

### Gaps
- I found no instance of Rukia casting kidō in TYBW manga fights (the Bleach Wiki TYBW history lists none). Absence is not proven for every page.
- I did not re-check every earlier dance use (the ch. 300 Shirafune variant, for example) against the manga pages themselves.

## 3. First appearance, appearance, anime staging, manga vs anime differences, iconic lines

### Takeaway
The Bankai is activated at the end of **manga ch. 569 "The White Haze"** (p16) and first shown in full at **ch. 570 pp6–7**. The anime adapts it in **ep. 385 (TYBW ep. 19, "THE WHITE HAZE")**, which aired 2023-08-12 in Japan, in the second cour 訣別譚. There the ending song "Endroll" plays as an insert over the Bankai. The **2026 fourth cour (相剋譚, The Calamity) adds an anime-original Bankai use against Yhwach in ep. 411 (TYBW ep. 45, 2026-08-22)**.

### Cited Findings
- **Manga sequence** (ch. 566–570):
  - Äs ambushes her and asks where "his Senbonzakura" is (ch. 566).
  - Her true-power reveal (ch. 567).
  - Äs's Vollständig Tatarforas traps her in a dome of eyes, fear through the optic nerve; Byakuya cuts her free (ch. 568).
  - Byakuya: 「強くなったな、ルキア」 ("You've grown strong, Rukia"); she sheds a tear; he leaves the fight to her; the Bankai is released in "a blinding pillar of white" (ch. 569 pp1–16).
  - Äs freezes and fades, her hand cracks, and Byakuya's warning follows (ch. 570 pp4–10).
  - — [Bleach Wiki: Rukia Kuchiki](https://bleach.fandom.com/wiki/Rukia_Kuchiki); the line quoted in Japanese by [ABEMA Times](https://times.abema.tv/articles/-/10091386?page=1)
- **Anime ep. 385** covers ch. 566 (pp6–17) through 570. It aired JP 2023-08-12 and EN 2023-09-09. — [Bleach Wiki: Episode 385](https://bleach.fandom.com/wiki/Episode_385)
- **Bankai appearance** (ch. 570 pp6–7 / ep. 385):
  - A white, ankle-length kimono with lined patterns, with collar, edges and shoulder pieces of **transparent ice** and long, wide sleeves.
  - Long ribbons tied at her back in many large loops.
  - A small ice flower at the centre of her chest.
  - A half-crown of ice around the back of her head.
  - **Hair and irises turn white.**
  - The blade keeps its Shikai shape but is made of transparent ice.
  - — [Bleach Wiki: Rukia Kuchiki](https://bleach.fandom.com/wiki/Rukia_Kuchiki)
  - The anime summary instead describes "an icy kimono with transparent adornments and **lilac hair**" — [Episode 385](https://bleach.fandom.com/wiki/Episode_385). ABEMA says her hair *and eyelashes* turned a translucent colour (「髪やまつ毛も透き通るような色に変化」) — [ABEMA Times](https://times.abema.tv/articles/-/10091386?page=1). So the anime colours the white hair with a pale lilac or translucent tint.
- **Anime staging:**
  - "Endroll" is used as an insert song "in lieu of the ending animation" over the end of the battle — [Episode 385](https://bleach.fandom.com/wiki/Episode_385).
  - Fans praised the timing of 折笠富美子's (Fumiko Orikasa, Rukia's voice actress) credit and the 「特殊EDからの卍解」 (Bankai straight out of the special ED) — [Oricon 2290788](https://www.oricon.co.jp/news/2290788/full/).
  - Asahi Production (the compositing/photography studio) posted that Rukia's Bankai and Äs's crumbling were done 「撮影で処理」, i.e. as compositing effects — quoted in the same [Oricon article](https://www.oricon.co.jp/news/2290788/full/).
  - The Bankai activation lands at about 20:20 into the episode — [ABEMA Times](https://times.abema.tv/articles/-/10091386?page=1).
- **Anime changes in ep. 385:**
  - Added: a demonic Äs face, a blood-dripping "F" that later **freezes and shatters**, and an extended Senbonzakura flashback.
  - Changed: Rukia's fear vision becomes friends rotting and spewing flies, instead of eyes turning black; she and Äs stay in place instead of moving down a level; Äs lands in front of her instead of behind her.
  - Removed: Rukia telling Renji to hide after each fight, and Äs mocking her for running from the thorns.
  - — [Bleach Wiki: Episode 385 (Anime Notes)](https://bleach.fandom.com/wiki/Episode_385)
- **ANIME-ORIGINAL (2026): ep. 411 "LIFE TO DEFEND YOU" (TYBW 45, JP 2026-08-22, EN 2026-09-26), adapting only ch. 677 pp1–7.**
  - Renji sends Rukia ahead.
  - Her thrown Shikai blade freezes Yhwach's darkness and briefly encases him.
  - She banters with Ichigo about his horn, "rolled up in a black blanket".
  - She fights beside him, freezing darkness on contact and freezing the ground to lock Yhwach down.
  - Yhwach remarks that "her power seems to restrain her ability to fight effectively alongside Ichigo."
  - During Blood Chains Ichigo, she activates Hakka no Togame while Ichigo's chains hold Yhwach, and freezes him "completely solid" for a point-blank Getsuga.
  - She is then impaled by a Reishi Broadsword.
  - — [Bleach Wiki: Episode 411](https://bleach.fandom.com/wiki/Episode_411)
  - Reviewers confirm that "The anime adding Rukia to this portion of the battle is one of several major changes from the manga" — [But Why Tho, ep. 45 review](https://butwhytho.net/2026/08/bleach-thousand-year-blood-war-episode-45/).
- **Personality in TYBW:** composed and fearless under Äs's fear attack. She turns it back on him and asks "what he fears". She defers to and is moved by Byakuya's approval, and accepts his caution about the Bankai. With Ichigo she keeps the old comic-rival banter even mid-apocalypse, as in the ep. 411 horn exchange. After the war she becomes Captain of the 13th Division, 10 years after Yhwach's defeat (ch. 685). — [Bleach Wiki: Rukia Kuchiki](https://bleach.fandom.com/wiki/Rukia_Kuchiki); [Episode 411](https://bleach.fandom.com/wiki/Episode_411)

  - Stating she is at −18 °C, where blood will not flow.
  - Byakuya's 「強くなったな、ルキア」.
  - The release 「卍解 白霞罸」.
  - Byakuya's "a blade wielded with the cost of the wielder's life will not be able to protect anything."
  - — [Episode 385](https://bleach.fandom.com/wiki/Episode_385); [Oricon](https://www.oricon.co.jp/news/2290788/full/); [ABEMA](https://times.abema.tv/articles/-/10091386?page=1)
- **Popularity:** Sode no Shirayuki placed 2nd in the first Zanpakutō poll and 1st in the second (the "Blades" databook) — [Bleach Wiki: Rukia Kuchiki](https://bleach.fandom.com/wiki/Rukia_Kuchiki). ### Inferences
- The awakening cinematic writes itself: a sword stance, then a white pillar from ground to sky with lake-like frost above and below, a wave off the blade tip, the target weathering into ice dust, and a close-up of the cracking hand. Palette: white kimono, clear ice trim, white or pale-lilac hair.
- The ep. 411 line "her power seems to restrain her ability to fight effectively alongside Ichigo" is a ready-made in-fiction justification for making the Bankai form *slower but area-dominant*.

### Gaps
- I could not verify the exact Japanese wording of Rukia's own ch. 567 lines (the 「死んでいる」 / "−18度" dialogue) from a primary source.
- Director or staff interviews about the ep. 385 staging: none found. Only the fan reactions and the Asahi Production post above.
- Volume number for ch. 569–570: the volume title is "HEAR, FEAR, HERE" per the wiki hatnote, but I did not confirm the number.

## 4. Game implementations and balance reception

### Takeaway
**Bleach: Rebirth of Souls (2025) does NOT have Bankai Rukia.** Its story stops at the end of the Arrancar arc, so her awakening is **Shikai: Sode no Shirayuki**, and her un-awakened kit is kidō (Shakkahō / Byakurai / Sōkatsui). The four DLC so far are TYBW Ichigo, Unohana, Ichibe and Yhwach; none is a TYBW Rukia. Hakka no Togame appears as a **one-shot special or super** in Brave Souls and Jump Force, and as a **timed at-will transformation** on Brave Souls' captain Rukia. Players rate RoS Rukia low-to-mid, and her weaknesses are short reach and slow startup.

### Cited Findings
- **RoS roster:** "Rukia Kuchiki (Awakening – Shikai release: Sode no Shirayuki)". The story runs from the first meeting to the end of the Arrancar arc. The DLC are Ichigo (TYBW), Ichibe, Unohana and Yhwach. — [Bleach Wiki: BLEACH Rebirth of Souls](https://bleach.fandom.com/wiki/BLEACH_Rebirth_of_Souls); [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bleach_Rebirth_of_Souls)
- **RoS official move list** (JP site):
  - Style: 「小回りが利き攻撃を仕掛けやすいキャラクター」 (nimble, easy to attack with).
  - 己有技 (signature): 破道の三十一『赤火砲』 (Hadō #31 Shakkahō). Tap and hold give Shakkahō with different power and timing.
  - 霊圧技1 (SP1): 破道の四『白雷』 (Hadō #4 Byakurai), with invincibility on activation.
  - 霊圧技2 (SP2): 破道の三十三『蒼火墜』 (Hadō #33 Sōkatsui). A forward lunge slash that follows up with Sōkatsui on hit.
  - 覚醒 (awakening): 始解「袖白雪」, which strengthens everything and **transforms her SP moves**.
  - — [BLEACH RoS official: 朽木ルキア](https://bleach-ros.bn-ent.net/character/?chara=rukia-kuchiki)
  - The English trailer text says Byakurai is "released while stepping backward… momentarily invincible" — [Anime Corner](https://animecorner.me/rukia-kuchiki-gets-bleach-rebirth-of-souls-character-trailer/). Note that the signature Shakkahō is itself anime-filler kidō for Rukia (see §2).
- **RoS patch 1.10 (Bandai Namco EU, dated 10/06/2025).**
  - Base form changes:
    - Special Flash Attack can now be cancelled into Quick and Flash, with more stun.
    - Combo scaling was raised to make extensions harder.
    - The Special Flash Attack no longer counter-hits.
    - Follow-up Hohō has more stun.
  - Awakened form changes:
    - Signature (charge) can be cancelled into Special Flash.
    - SP1 is scaled harder and can only cancel into Special Flash or SP2 on hit; step-cancel only on hit.
    - **SP2 gets more slow effect and stun (third stage only)**.

## 5. Design hooks: awakened vs non-awakened as different playstyles, not different power (for SOUL DUEL)

### Takeaway
Canon offers a clean split. **Shikai Rukia** is a mobile *placer*: circles, waves, ice lines and kidō, fighting at mid range. **Hakka no Togame Rukia** is a still *field* that gets colder over time: slow movement, freeze on contact, immunity to status and contact effects at the bottom of the temperature ladder, and a self-damage or thaw cost. SOUL DUEL awakenings are permanent (DUEL_DESIGN §1), so the canon time limit becomes a *cycle inside the form* rather than a form that runs out.

### Cited Findings (canon anchors for each hook)
- Placed-circle trap: Tsukishiro freezes everything in the drawn circle, pillar included, and the target shatters "shortly after" (a delay). — [Bleach Wiki](https://bleach.fandom.com/wiki/Rukia_Kuchiki)
- Charged wave: Hakuren has a four-stab setup before the release. — [Bleach Wiki](https://bleach.fandom.com/wiki/Rukia_Kuchiki)
- Reach extension: Shirafune's ice blade keeps freezing the area after it pierces. — [Bleach Wiki](https://bleach.fandom.com/wiki/Rukia_Kuchiki)
- Ground trail (anime-only): Juhaku. — [Bleach Wiki: Juhaku](https://bleach.fandom.com/wiki/Juhaku)
- Temperature stages: −18 °C stops bleeding, −50 °C causes an ice quake, −273 °C lasts ≤4 s, and she must thaw slowly afterwards, with a cut or crack if she overstays. — [Episode 385](https://bleach.fandom.com/wiki/Episode_385)
- At absolute zero, contact or entering effects fail but optical ones still work. — [Bleach Wiki](https://bleach.fandom.com/wiki/Rukia_Kuchiki)
- Anyone who touches her freezes: Byakuya's fingers frost over. — [Episode 385](https://bleach.fandom.com/wiki/Episode_385)
- Mobility limited at first. — [Bleach Wiki](https://bleach.fandom.com/wiki/Rukia_Kuchiki)
- Anime-original: she freezes the ground to pin Yhwach, and a Bankai freeze holds a bound enemy for an ally. — [Episode 411](https://bleach.fandom.com/wiki/Episode_411)
- RoS precedent: SP2 applies a *slow* effect in the awakened form. — [Patch 1.10](https://en.bandainamcoent.eu/bleach/news/bleach-rebirth-of-souls-patch-version-110)

### Inferences (proposals; not canon)
- **Base (Shikai, 舞) = trap and zone dancer.**
  - J/K strings are quick and short-reached, faithful to RoS's "小回り" and to the canon Shunpo expertise.
  - Signature: **Tsukishiro**. Draw a circle at a chosen distance; after a delay (~20–30 f) a pillar freezes anyone inside. A trap and anti-approach tool that is visible and avoidable.
  - SP1: **Hakuren**. The four-stab setup is the windup; the wave has long range and slows or freezes.
  - SP2: **Shirafune**. A lunge whose extended ice blade leaves a frozen patch that slows.
  - Optional kidō flavour (Sōkatsui, Byakurai as the invulnerable backstep from RoS) can fill the Flash or Breaker slots.
  - Plays like a mid-range controller that wins by where it puts ice.
- **Awakened (白霞罸) = stance / field / tempo.**
  - A **temperature meter**. Standing still or holding a "stillness" input cools her through −18 / −50 / −273.
  - Each stage adds a trait:
    - −18: hits cause no bleed or chip on her; she stops chip damage.
    - −50: an ice-quake ground pulse around her.
    - −273: a ≤4 s (240 f) window of immunity to contact, status and throws, where anyone who touches her or blocks her freezes. Projectiles and "gaze" moves still work, as canon's optical loophole.
  - Movement speed drops as she gets colder.
  - The O ender or Kikon at −273 is the ground-to-sky pillar plus the blade-tip wave.
  - Leaving −273 forces a **thaw**: slowly warming up with reduced action, or a fast thaw at an HP cost shown as a hand-crack VFX.
  - Overstaying past 4 s deals self-damage, per the canon cut on her thumb.
  - Same total strength, different verb. The base form *places* ice and moves; the awakened form *is* the ice, rooted and area-denying, and trades mobility for a lockdown field.
- **Clarity and anti-frustration:** telegraph the temperature stage on her model (breath frost, then ice trim, then white hair) and in the HUD, so opponents know when to back off or zone her. That respects the canon weakness of being slow and the "optic" loophole.
- **Awakening cinematic beats** (canon-true): Byakuya-style stance, 「卍解 白霞罸」, a white pillar with lake-like frost above and below, the tip wave, the target crumbling to ice dust, and a close-up of the cracking hand before the thaw.
- **Flag for the user:** a Bankai that moves freely at full speed is supported only by the 2026 anime-original ep. 411 and Brave Souls' captain version. The manga supports a slow, rooted Bankai. That favours the stance/field reading.
