# NINJA GAIDEN 4 — research notes (reference for RAVEN EDGE)

Researched 2026-09. Sources are cited inline as [S#] and listed at the end.
Markers: **[confirmed]** = official site or several sources agree. **[secondary]** = one guide or review.
**[uncertain]** = our inference or general series knowledge, not verified for NG4.

## Facts
- Developed by Team Ninja and PlatinumGames, published by Xbox Game Studios. Released 2025-10-21 on PS5, Xbox Series X|S and PC. [S2] **[confirmed]**
- Two playable characters. **Yakumo** of the Raven Clan is the new protagonist. **Ryu Hayabusa** is also playable. [S1][S2][S3] **[confirmed]**
- Yakumo's starting weapon is *Takeminakata*, a pair of katanas and a fast all-rounder. The other weapons are a rapier/lance (*Yatousen*), a staff/hammer (*Magatsuhi*) and mechanical assassin arms (*Kage-Hiruko*). Each weapon has a Bloodraven variant. [S1][S9] **[confirmed]**

## Core combat loop
- **Bloodraven Form.** Press L2 and Yakumo turns blood into a larger weapon. In this form every regular input performs a different move. It spends the **Bloodbind Gauge** and drains it fast. [S1][S3][S5] **[confirmed]**
  - How the gauge fills: over time, when Base Form attacks hit, and through Obliterations. Obliterations fill it by a large amount. [S5-search, S4] **[secondary]**
  - Most enemies cannot block Bloodraven attacks. A Bloodraven hit on a blocking enemy staggers it. [S5-search][S4] **[secondary]**
  - **Counter-play against Power Attacks** (the key interaction). A Bloodraven attack that lands during an enemy's Power Attack cancels the attack and staggers the enemy. The stagger lasts longer the closer the enemy was to releasing the attack. In Bloodraven Form you can also block Power Attacks at a gauge cost (L2+R2). The Xbox preview names a counter move, "Hōgeki", that deflects attacks and staggers armoured foes. [search snippet of S10][S3][S4] **[secondary]**
- **Power Attacks / red telegraph.** An enemy about to use a Power Attack glows **red**. It will not flinch, and Base Form hits cannot interrupt it. Blocking one in Base Form breaks your guard. [search snippet of S10][S4] **[secondary]**
- **Guard.** Hold R2/RT. Guard absorbs several hits and then breaks. An orange aura warns that the break is close. [S4] **[secondary]**
- **Fatal Flash.** A parry-and-counter that rewards anticipating an attack. [S1][S6] **[confirmed name; exact timing unknown]**
- **Obliteration Technique.** Press Heavy on a *dismembered* (limb-severed) enemy to kill it instantly. A heavy hit on a weakened enemy reliably severs a limb. [S1][S4][S6] **[confirmed]**
- **Ultimate Technique.** Hold Heavy to charge and release for a strong attack with brief invulnerability. Absorbing Blood Essence orbs charges it faster. [S1][S4] **[confirmed]**
- **Berserk State / Bloodbath Kill.** A Berserk gauge fills from dealing hits, taking damage and landing Obliterations. In Berserk, some Bloodraven attacks become a "Bloodbath Kill" that cuts enemies in half, and "Bloodbath Slaughter" executes every enemy in range. Ryu's version is True Dragon Sword / True Dragon's Gleam. [S1][S3][S7] **[confirmed]**
- **Series moves kept in NG4.** Izuna Drop grabs an enemy you have launched, slams it into the ground and damages nearby enemies. Flying Swallow is a mid-air slash that travels to a target; enemies can counter it if you overuse it. Wind Path springboards off an enemy. [S1][S4][S5] **[confirmed]**
- **Enemy armour.** Enemies glowing white shrug off basic hits, so you need Bloodraven attacks. A metallic clang means you should switch to Bloodraven. [S4] **[secondary]**
- **Shuriken cancel.** Throwing a shuriken cancels the tail of a long string. [S4] **[secondary]**

## Enemies, aggression, bosses
- Reviewers stress large numbers of foes ("scores of foes"), unblockable assaults and long fights. Common complaints are limited enemy variety and battles that run long. [S7] **[secondary]**
- 1-v-1 fights exist against opponents who block. You must time your openings. [S5] **[secondary]**
- Bosses are "creatively designed" demons. Ryu's chapters reuse some of them. [S7] **[secondary]**
- *Series staple, not verified for NG4* **[uncertain]**: several enemies attack at once and punish recovery frames, and severed enemies make desperate suicide attacks, which pushes you to finish them fast. [S8]

## Camera, feel, presentation
- The feel is fast and weighty with "linear" movement. The director says the game goes "the complete opposite direction" from the trend toward easier games. [S3] **[secondary]**
- Critics call the camera and targeting "annoying at times" but fine most of the time. [search summary of S11] **[secondary]**
- Hitstop, slow motion on Obliteration and camera cuts during finishers are all visible in footage, but we found no written source. **[uncertain]**
- **Setting.** Near-future Tokyo corrupted by the Dark Dragon. "Cursed" or "miasma" rain turns the city into a rainy cyberpunk space that overlaps the Underworld. The director names "adversity" as the core theme. The game has rail grinding and collapsing buildings. [S2][S5] **[confirmed]**
- **HUD.** No source describes it. From memory: health and gauges top-left, and a boss bar. **[uncertain]**

## Difficulty
- The game has **Hero** mode (assists, aimed at newcomers), **Normal** and **Hard**, with harder modes unlocked later. [S6] **[secondary]**

## General NG-series staples (from Ninja Gaiden / NG2)
- **Dismemberment → Obliteration.** A crippled enemy is weaker but still dangerous and may make suicide attacks. Kill it with a finisher. [S8]
- **Essence.** Coloured orbs drop from kills. In NG2 they restore health or ki, or give currency. Drawing essence in charges the Ultimate Technique. [S8]
- **Reverse Wind** is the series' evasive roll (NG2 names it). [S8] Wall run and wall jump ("Flying Bird Flip") are core Ryu movement in NG (2004) and NG2 **[uncertain for NG4 details]**.
- **Chiburui.** A blood-flick with the sword after combat. [S8]

## Takeaways for RAVEN EDGE
1. The gauge-fuelled transform is the answer to red attacks. That rock-paper-scissors (dodge them, or break them with the gauge) is NG4's newest idea, and we copy the structure under original names.
2. Crippling a limb makes a finisher prompt appear. This is simple with rigid-part rigs: detach the arm part.
3. Enemies are aggressive, and guard breaks punish turtling.
4. Keep the Izuna-style slam and the Flying-Swallow-style dive: these are the series' signature moves.
