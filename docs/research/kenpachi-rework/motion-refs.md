KATATE / RYOTE / NOMIHOSE, Bankai oni / KATAUDE) so that each strike has its own silhouette and whole-body motion.

## 1. Canon: how he fights, fight by fight

### 1.1 General style (Bleach Wiki "Powers & Abilities"; A/C)
- He fights by instinct and has "no set pattern of attacks". His arsenal is "overhead, sweeps, thrusts, or jabs". He is
  **ambidextrous** and "always fight[s] with one hand free, only using both hands when he feels not doing so would lead
  to his defeat, such as against Nnoitra and momentarily Ichigo." (C, wiki prose)
- He is an expert brawler: he "can easily catch an opponent's attack with a single hand, perform powerful punches and
  kicks, and easily subdue opponents with throws and take-downs." (C)
- Kendō: Yamamoto forced him to learn it, but the training lasted only a day. The one lesson he kept is that "a blade
  swung with both arms is more powerful than with one". The technique is **Ryōdan 両断 "Bisection"**: "Grasping their
  sword with both hands, the practitioner brings it down with enough force to cut an opponent in half down the middle"
  (wiki `Ryōdan`, citing ch. 311 pp. 17–19). (A/C)
- Before Unohana he had **no Shunpo**. His speed came from raw leg strength, "fast enough to instantly close a huge gap"
  (a straight-line burst, not a teleport). After Unohana he used Shunpo-level rushes. (C)
- His sword was carried "across his back by a rope, over his right shoulder" as a wanderer, and later at the left of his
  waist. It is about nodachi length, notched and chipped, and bandaged at the hilt. (C)

### 1.2 vs Ichigo, Soul Society arc (ch. ~104–115; A)
- **"Cut me anywhere."** He bares his chest, unguarded, and lets Ichigo cut him. Ichigo's hands bleed from the recoil.
  He then "pushes Ichigo back, using his arm against his blade" (a forearm shove into the sword).
- **Pursuit.** He bursts through a wall in front of the fleeing Ichigo. His first strike is a **downward hack Ichigo
  barely blocks**, the blade pressing down and inching closer. When Ichigo slips it, the blade **slams into and ruptures
  the ground**.
- **Bored.** He **sits down against a wall** waiting, then stands up when Ichigo returns (an idle / taunt reference).
- **Blade-dragging run-up**: "Ichigo and Kenpachi drag their Zanpakutō through a nearby building and the ground,
  respectively, before clashing in an explosion of force." The tip trails on the floor through the approach and comes
  up into the clash.
- **Blade grab and twist**: "a grinning Kenpachi grabs Zangetsu by the blade and twists it around, pulling Ichigo off his
  feet and into the air, as he thrusts his Zanpakutō forward." The left hand wrenches while the right hand stabs.
- **Behind-you slash.** He vanishes and strikes from behind. Ichigo blocks overhead because he hears the bells.
- **The thrust through the sword**: "Kenpachi stabs his Zanpakutō right through Zangetsu and into Ichigo's chest." He
  then withdraws, turns away disappointed, and is "**dragging his Zanpakutō through the ground and swinging it onto his
  shoulder**" (the canon walk-away, a signature carry).
- **Momentary two hands**: overpowered by the revived Ichigo, he "prepares to grip his Zanpakutō's handle with his other
  hand". He is sent skidding back and **stabs the sword into a wall to stop himself**.
- **The laughing lunge**: "leaping toward Ichigo … allows the tip of the blade to slash through the bridge of his nose
  and across his left cheek … so he can get close and jab his Zanpakutō". He takes the hit on purpose to close the gap,
  then rushes in again laughing.
- **Eyepatch off**: he rips it off, his reiatsu blasts up "in a column wreathed in a helix", and he "**casually flicks
  his Zanpakutō diagonally across the base of the building next to him**", which falls. The motion is tiny and the
  result huge.
- **Final clash**: both exert reiatsu, his as a **skull-shaped aura**, and "rush toward each other and clash in an
  explosion". The result: Zangetsu is buried in his right shoulder and **his blade has impaled Ichigo's abdomen** (a
  running thrust or stab, not a cut). His blade snaps in two.

### 1.3 vs Tōsen and Komamura (SS arc; A/C)
- Blinded by Suzumushi's Bankai, he lets Tōsen **impale him, grabs Suzumushi's hilt** to hold him there, and cuts him.
- **Komamura blocks a slash from Zaraki, which shatters his helmet** (the shockwave goes through the block).

### 1.4 vs Nnoitra, Hueco Mundo (ch. 307–312; A)
- One fatal slash down Tesla's chest. He **kicks Ichigo** out of the way ("you're in the way").
- An **upward slash launches Nnoitra into the air**.
- **Chain yank and head slam**: he sidesteps the thrown weapon, **jams his own sword blade-first into the sand**, grabs
  the chain, pulls Nnoitra down, "**grabbing Nnoitra by the head and slamming him on his back** into the sands", then
  re-grabs his sword and **stabs down at the head**.
- **Catches a weapon by the blade** and holds it back, then **thrusts through Nnoitra's eyepatch** and out the back of
  his head. Nnoitra ends up hanging off his sword.
- He grabs Nnoitra's kicking leg out of the air, laughing.
- **Catches a Cero with his right hand and throws it aside**, where it detonates behind him.
- **Upward slash to the left shoulder**. When the eyepatch is ripped off, "a stunned Nnoitra is **slashed down the
  length of his body**".
- From inside rubble, he **kicks his foot out of the debris**, grabs Nnoitra's head, **slams him onto his back** and
  brings the sword down in "a powerful slash", which Nnoitra blocks with all four scythes.
- **Ryōdan**: "grips the bottom of his Zanpakutō's handle with his left hand, and performs Ryōdan, a powerful downward
  slash" that "cuts through Nnoitra along his left side and **kicks up a huge plume of sand as it travels across the
  desert**". He then "stands with both hands on his Zanpakutō" in the finished pose, turns and walks off.
  - The left hand goes on the **pommel end** (A). Whether the wind-up is a jōdan overhead or a lift from chūdan is not
    stated in the text (D: my memory of the panel is a high two-handed lift, then the drop).
- He finishes Nnoitra with "another blow to his chest" when Nnoitra charges again.

### 1.5 vs Yammy (A/C)
- "**Severs a released Yammy's massive leg with a single slash**" and knocks him off his feet. He takes a giant's punch
  into a pillar and walks out unfazed.

### 1.6 vs Unohana, TYBW (ch. 524–527; anime TYBW ep. 9–10; A/C)
- Eyepatch off from the start, fighting **one-handed**. Unohana mocks "someone fighting with one arm not making use of the
  other", stabs **daggers** into his shoulder, **disarms** him (the sword spins up and sticks in the ground), pins him to
  a pillar and stabs him through the throat.
- Flashback: young Kenpachi **leaps off a mountain of corpses**, overpowers her, and **stabs into the center of her
  chest between the clavicles**.
- Present: he "**lands across from Unohana and … cut[s] her left arm with a slash**". She **dodges back from "a powerful
  downward strike … that shakes the ground upon impact"**, and he "**slashes his Zanpakutō down the length of her body
  with tremendous force**" (a full-height vertical cut). He blocks her overhead cut **perpendicularly** (blade
  horizontal over his head).
- Her Bankai: he "**roars with glee and rushes toward her**", laughing madly. The finish is a **stab through her chest**.
  He pulls the sword out and **drops it**.

### 1.7 vs Gremmy, Shikai debut (ch. 574–578; anime TYBW ep. 20 "I Am The Edge", 2023-08-19; A/C)
- He **breaks through a wall** to arrive, charges, and is stopped by imagined skin. He cuts through anyway, then cuts
  away lava, steel columns and a beam that blocks his sword. He emerges from a hail of machine-gun fire with minor
  wounds.
- **Meteor**: "looks up at the meteor with unrestrained glee … **launches himself into the air toward the meteor before
  releasing his Shikai** … cuts completely through the center of the meteor, splitting it in half". Then "**In the air
  above Gremmy, Kenpachi stands with Nozarashi … resting on his shoulder.**" (A). The chapter's title is 刃 "Blade".
- In the vacuum he cuts himself out by **bifurcating a clone** (a whole-body top-down cut).
- Anime: "a very fast back and forth … Zaraki cuts through steel, meteors, and eventually the vacuum of space"
  (Inbetween Drafts, C). I found no per-cut choreography breakdown in text. **Gap: watch ep. 20 for the cut timings.**
- Nozarashi Shikai look: "an **axe/war cleaver hybrid twice his size with a long, cloth-wrapped handle**. The top of the
  blade has a brass cover with a **green tassel**" (wiki Shikai section). The same wiki's ch. 577 summary says "a large
  **red** tassel". **The sources conflict**: check the colour page before you paint it.

### 1.8 vs Pernida (TYBW; A)
- He **slashes it across the head** (drawing blood) and takes a slash to the arm. When the arm starts warping, he
  **rips his own right arm off**, then rushes forward and slashes partway through Pernida before Mayuri paralyzes him.

### 1.9 vs Gerard, Bankai (ch. 668–671; anime TYBW "The Calamity" ep. 4 "The Perfect Crimson", 2026-08-15; A/C)
- Shikai phase: he **cuts off the giant's forearm** on arrival. He **grabs Gerard's stomping foot and pushes or throws
  him back** so the giant crashes into a tower. He "**leaps after Gerard**" and is blown back by the shield. He releases
  Nozarashi and **blocks Gerard's enormous slash**, "manages to **repel** Gerard and **leaps into the air**", then takes
  the reflected cut. He **smashes Hitsugaya to the ground** to keep him out of the fight.
- **Bankai** (A): an energy wave, red skin, horns, **irisless eyes** (C: Naledir review of the anime), and a shorter,
  jagged, **broken cleaver**. In order:
  1. Gerard punches. Kenpachi "**bit[es] down on Gerard's knuckle and rip[s] the whole arm off**".
  2. "**Leaps into the air before slashing at Gerard**", cutting off the left forearm along with **half of his shield**.
  3. "**Punches Gerard in the chin**, sending him flying back off the edge of Wahrwelt".
  4. Gerard flies back in Vollständig. Kenpachi "**swiftly cuts him in half vertically**".
  5. Bestial: he "**leaps back and roars**" as the halves rise. Byakuya and Hitsugaya keep away "due to his bestial
     mannerisms".
  6. He prepares to resume and "**his right arm suddenly shatters and breaks open**". Gerard slashes him into the ground.
  7. Badly injured, he still "**flip[s] Gerard over**" (trips the giant by grabbing and pushing his feet) "and mutilate[s]
     his feet".
- Anime: "**swings his half-cleaver with the force of a creature that cannot be reasoned with**", "attack patterns become
  insanely unpredictable", and his voice gains a multi-tone distortion (Naledir, C). He "fights as much with teeth as with
  his broken sword" (search snippet of a review, C).
- In Bankai he is a berserker who attacks allies after the bisection (wiki "Weaknesses", C).

## 3. Motion bars for SOUL DUEL

Each line gives the archetype, a one-line body description, and the source it is drawn from. The **slot** column is only
a suggestion against the current kit (J string, K heavies, L stance, Shift+K SP1, Shift+L SP2, I Breaker, O Kikon). The
rule across all three forms is that **no two strikes share an arc plane and a hand set**. Vary these between strikes:
the plane (vertical / diagonal / horizontal / rising / thrust), the hands (R / L-free grab / both / fist / teeth), the
travel (step / lunge / leap / spin / planted), and the end pose.

### 3.1 Sealed: the notched katana, one hand, wild

**Idle / stance.** Weight sunk on the back (right) leg, shoulders rolled forward, head tipped down so he looks up from
under the brow, grinning. The katana hangs loosely from the right hand with the tip near the floor behind-right
(dragging), **or** sits on the right shoulder (A: both carries). The left arm hangs open and free, the hand half-curled,
ready to grab (A: one hand always free). Optional bored idle: settles back and **sits against a wall** (A).
**Walk.** A slow heavy swagger, rolling shoulders, the head forward, the blade tip **scraping the floor behind**
(A: ch. 114–115), or on the shoulder. **Run.** Bent forward, long strides, the blade either on the shoulder (as built) or
**trailing low behind with the tip in the ground** (A), coming up into the first strike.

### 3.2 Nozarashi: the cleaver "twice his size" (a three-cup ladder)
The cleaver changes everything. Its **mass leads and the body follows**: longer wind-ups, the follow-through carries him a
step, and recovery means hauling it back to the shoulder. At rest it **sits on the right shoulder** (A: ch. 577). Grip
reference: "a long, cloth-wrapped handle" (A/C), long enough for two hands.

**Idle (all cups).** The cleaver on the right shoulder with the blade behind him, the left hand free, a wide stance, the
grin. **Walk.** Slower than sealed; the cleaver bobs on the shoulder. **Run.** The cleaver on the shoulder (as built), or
