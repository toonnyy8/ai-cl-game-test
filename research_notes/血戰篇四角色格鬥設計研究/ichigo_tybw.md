# TYBW Kurosaki Ichigo (dual true Zangetsu) and 「血鎖の一護」: fact file for a SOUL DUEL character

Scope: canon kit and characterisation, verification of the user's 「血鎖斬月」, game implementations, and design hooks for an awakening that changes playstyle rather than power. As of 2026-09-27. Legend: **[MANGA]** canon in both manga and anime, **[ANIME-ORIG]** anime-only, **[UNVERIFIED]** from a search snippet or a single weak source.

## 1. Canon moveset and characterisation of TYBW dual-blade Ichigo (manga vs anime)

### Takeaway
In TYBW Ichigo's Zanpakutō is reforged by Nimaiya Ōetsu into a **pair of blades**: a long cleaver with a hole through it and a hiltless short blade. His signature Shikai technique becomes **Getsuga Jūjishō**, two Getsuga Tenshō fused into a cross. The true Bankai **Tensa Zangetsu** is made by overlaying the two blades. Yhwach breaks it the instant it is released, and Tsukishima and Orihime restore it. The manga never shows a full fight with the true Bankai. The anime's Part 4 fills that gap with the anime-original 血鎖の一護 (section 2).

### Cited Findings
**Reforging and the two blades [MANGA]**
- The true Zangetsu became a "二刀一対型" (paired twin-blade type). The long blade is "始解に似た形状で、柄と刀身の中央に穴がある" (shaped like the old Shikai, with a hole between the hilt and the blade). The short blade "柄が無く石包丁に似た形状" (has no hilt and looks like a stone knife). An official Q&A says the old sheathless Zangetsu was "斬魄刀としては正しい形ではなく" (not the correct form for a Zanpakutō), and the reforging "正しい形に直した" (put it back into the correct form) — [ja.wikipedia 黒崎一護](https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E9%BB%92%E5%B4%8E%E4%B8%80%E8%AD%B7)
- The two swords "each reflect the Quincy and Hollow aspects of his soul, allowing Ichigo to be in perfect harmony with Zangetsu", forged by Nimaiya Ōetsu in the Royal Realm — [CBR](https://www.cbr.com/bleach-ichigo-final-zanpakuto-explained/)
- Anime: TYBW **episode 13 "THE BLADE IS ME"** (Part 1 finale, 2022). Ichigo learns that the "Old Man Zangetsu" looks like a young Yhwach and is his Quincy power, which had been secretly limiting him. When he pulls his blade from the forge it comes out as TWO blades — [Bleach Wiki ep. page via search](https://bleach.fandom.com/wiki/THE_BLADE_IS_ME_(episode)), [Game Rant review](https://gamerant.com/bleach-thousand-year-blood-war-episode-13-review/)

**Identity reveal: the heritage that drives the kit [MANGA]**
- 「斬月のオッサン」 is "死神の力ではなく一護の滅却師の力" (not Shinigami power but Ichigo's Quincy power), and his "姿は千年前のユーハバッハを形取" (took the form of Yhwach from a thousand years ago). 「白一護」 (the inner Hollow) is "死神の力を司る存在" (the being that governs his Shinigami power), born when the modified Hollow White fused with that power — [ja.wikipedia](https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E9%BB%92%E5%B4%8E%E4%B8%80%E8%AD%B7)
- Design implication: the canon ties the **big blade to the Hollow/Shinigami side** and the **short blade to the Quincy side**. CBR's wording is "Quincy and Hollow aspects". Which blade maps to which side is usually a fan reading; none of the fetched sources states it as fact. See Gaps.

**Techniques**
- **Getsuga Jūjishō (月牙十字衝, "Moon Fang Cross Blast")** [MANGA]. He fires a Getsuga Tenshō from each blade and fuses them into one large cross-shaped blast. Against Yhwach, a single Jūjishō dispersed the "ultimate defense spell" Kirchenlied: Sankt Zwinger and wounded Yhwach at the same time — [Bleach Wiki Getsuga Jūjishō via search snippet](https://bleach.fandom.com/wiki/Getsuga_J%C5%ABjish%C5%8D) (the page itself was paywalled with a 402, so this rests on the snippet)
- Anime: Jūjishō in TYBW **Part 2 ep. 8** against Candice Catnipp and the Bambies, while saving Kenpachi. It caused "a blinding explosion that engulfs the area" and took off one of Candice's arms — [DualShockers](https://www.dualshockers.com/bleach-tybw-ichigo-kurosaki-uses-getsuga-jujisho-for-the-first-time-in-the-series/). Note: Part 2 ep. 8 is roughly overall ep. 21. DualShockers calls it the "first time in the series", but the move already existed in the Fullbring arc; this looks like the first **anime** use with the dual blades.
- **Half-Hollowfication (半虚化)** [MANGA]. It comes out during the fight because Ichigo "虚の力を自在に使いこなせなかった" (could not wield his Hollow power freely). He gets the mask's markings and a horn on the **left side** of his face — [ja.wikipedia](https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E9%BB%92%E5%B4%8E%E4%B8%80%E8%AD%B7)

**True Bankai: Tensa Zangetsu (天鎖斬月) [MANGA]**
- It is released by "2対の斬月を重ね合わせて" (overlaying the pair of Zangetsu). The result is "白い刀身に中央辺りから柄にかけて黒の太いラインが引かれ" (a white blade with a thick black line running from about the middle down to the hilt) — [ja.wikipedia](https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E9%BB%92%E5%B4%8E%E4%B8%80%E8%AD%B7)
- Yhwach breaks it "解放と同時に" (at the moment of release). Tsukishima's Book of the End "折られなかった過去を挟ま" (inserts a past in which it was not broken), which restores it — [ja.wikipedia](https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E9%BB%92%E5%B4%8E%E4%B8%80%E8%AD%B7); same sequence (Orihime's Sōten Kisshun rejects the broken state, Ichigo stands with his Bankai "and his confidence completely restored") — [Geeky Inc / search summary](https://www.geekyinc.com/ichigo-vs-yhwach-explained-how-bleachs-final-fight-ends/)
- Approximate chapters **[UNVERIFIED]**: Bankai broken and power absorbed about ch. 679–680, restored about ch. 681, decisive strike about ch. 683–685 — [search summary citing Geeky Inc](https://www.geekyinc.com/ichigo-vs-yhwach-explained-how-bleachs-final-fight-ends/). CBR puts the moment when the reconstructed Bankai "deteriorates to reveal his original Shikai underneath" in **ch. 684**. The old single cleaver lands the final blow after Yhwach's power flickers out, and the manga never explains how it was "hidden inside" the Bankai — [CBR](https://www.cbr.com/bleach-ichigo-final-zanpakuto-explained/)
- One merchandise blog claims that at the true Bankai "he inserts the Quincy short blade into the hollowed section of the long blade… golden spiritual pressure erupts… a massive black-and-white greatsword" — [CoolKatana](https://www.coolkatana.com/blogs/news/ichigo-s-true-bankai-explained-why-zangetsu-returned-in-bleach-tybw). **Low-reliability source.** The short-blade-in-the-hole detail roughly matches ja.wikipedia's "overlaying". The "golden" colour is unconfirmed.
- Epilogue: Ichigo keeps his original single Shikai, and the dual blades do not appear again — [CBR](https://www.cbr.com/bleach-ichigo-final-zanpakuto-explained/)

### Inferences
- Canon beats that suit a fighter: (a) two blades with different reach (long cleaver plus short blade) suggest a two-button split, big-blade heavy against short-blade quick; (b) the dual Getsuga that fuses into a cross is a natural two-stage or charge move; (c) half-Hollowfication with the one-sided horn suits a mid-match "loss of control, gain of ferocity" state; (d) "Bankai shattered, then restored" and "original Shikai under the Bankai" are ready-made cinematic and Kikon moments.
- Characterisation in the arc: Ichigo protects, plays the underdog, and accepts his heritage. His fights are straightforward rushdown with huge single Getsuga payoffs rather than technical tricks. The arc's emotional core is Ichigo becoming one with his blade ("THE BLADE IS ME").

- The exact manga chapter numbers are approximate (search summary only).
- Which blade is "Hollow" and which is "Quincy" is not stated explicitly in any fetched source.

## 2. What exactly is 「血鎖斬月」? (verification)

### Takeaway
**There is no official form called 「血鎖斬月」.** The closest match is the anime-original form **「血鎖の一護」 (けっさのいちご, *Kessa no Ichigo*; English "Blood Chains Ichigo")**, which Tite Kubo designed and named himself. It first appears in **TYBW ep. 45 「DEFEND YOU」** (Part 4 "禍進譚 / The Calamity", ep. 5), aired **2026-08-22 (JST)**. It is not a Bankai name: it is the state of Ichigo after he half-Hollowfies and then releases Bankai. The user's 「血鎖斬月」 looks like a blend of 血鎖 and 天鎖斬月, and nothing official matches it.

## 4. Design hooks: awakened vs non-awakened as different playstyles, not different power

### Takeaway
Canon gives a clean contrast. **Base = dual-blade Shikai (Quincy + Hollow in balance, close-range two-blade footsies, Getsuga/Jūjishō branch).** **Awakened = 血鎖の一護 (chains and clones: space control, multi-angle pressure, a lingering crescent).** Each side gives up something, so neither is simply stronger.

### Cited Findings
- SOUL DUEL systems (from the repo's DUEL_DESIGN.md): J/K 3-link strings, O Kikon rush (count 2, or 3 in an awakened form), guard gauge, Reiatsu 3 bars for SP1/SP2, a separate flash-step gauge, one awakening per match lasting to the end, precedent for form-specific U (Yamamoto West ward, Kenpachi DRINK) — [docs/DUEL_DESIGN.md](/media/8tsp/projects/ai-cl-game-test/docs/DUEL_DESIGN.md)
- Canon anchors: Jūjishō = two Getsuga fused into a cross — [Bleach Wiki snippet](https://bleach.fandom.com/wiki/Getsuga_J%C5%ABjish%C5%8D); 血鎖: chains block attacks and control the battlefield, reiatsu clones, enormous Getsuga — [FandomWire](https://fandomwire.com/bleach-tybw-blood-chains-ichigo-bankai-form-explained/); chain reiatsu on the neck, hands and feet, two horns, no mask — [電撃オンライン](https://dengekionline.com/article/202608/85246)

### Inferences (design proposals, not sourced facts)
- **Base (二刀の斬月, Shikai)**: a close-to-mid "two-blade rhythm" rushdown. J = short Quincy blade (fast and short, low guard drain) and K = big cleaver (slow, long, high guard drain), so string order becomes the choice. SP = Getsuga Tenshō, with Jūjishō as a **two-press cross** (fire one Getsuga, press again within a window to fire the second and fuse them) that breaks guard or pierces a projectile, as a nod to Sankt Zwinger. Strength: burst, guard pressure, straightforward. Weakness: little space control.
- **Awakened (血鎖の一護)**: spacing and control. The chains become a mid-range **pull or bind** tool (O-ender or a U replacement, in line with the Yamamoto/Kenpachi precedent of a form-specific U: for example **U = CHAIN**, a chain parry or catch that blocks and then yanks the opponent in). **Clones**: a Flash Step that leaves a delayed afterimage slash, giving multi-angle mixups. **Getsuga** leaves a **crescent afterimage hitbox** that lingers and blocks space. For balance: lower per-hit damage and guard drain than the base form, and chain and clone moves cost Reiatsu, so it trades raw burst for control.
- **Thematic tie-in for the Kikon**: base Kikon = dual Getsuga into Jūjishō; awakened Kikon = chains bind Yhwach-style, then a giant blood-red crescent. The "Bankai broken, then restored" beat could be a cinematic flourish on awakening (blade shatters, then reforms with chains), which is **anime-inspired and not a canon sequence**.
- Avoid RoS's approach of awakening as a pure frame or hit buff, which breaks the user's rule.
- Name: label the awakening in-game as 血鎖の一護 / BLOOD CHAINS (canon). If the user wants 「血鎖斬月」 as a move or mode name, document it as a SOUL DUEL coinage.

### Gaps
- There are no canon frame-level details of the 血鎖 fight to copy. The mechanics above are extrapolated from the three canon ability descriptions (chains, clones, giant Getsuga).
